# UNCTAD — United Nations Conference on Trade and Development

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

(Now branded "UN Trade and Development"; still UNCTAD in the UN system.)

## Systems

- **Meetings archive** (Meeting documents) — <https://unctad.org/meetings> — browsable by body and session; each session page carries its outcome documents (agreed conclusions, decisions, reports). Conference and TDB outcomes also issue as UN documents (TD/… and TD/B/… symbols) in the official-document repository and UN ODS.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — TDB decisions (e.g. Decision 529 (LXIII)), commission agreed conclusions, and quadrennial Conference outcomes (the Bridgetown Covenant, UNCTAD16, Oct 2025) are published and findable via the meetings archive and the official-document repository |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded registry; each quadrennial Conference outcome resets the four-year mandate framework (Doha Mandate → Nairobi Maafikiano → Bridgetown Covenant) but there is no per-decision status system |
| 3 budget-linked | no — UNCTAD is funded through the UN regular programme budget (Section 12) plus extrabudgetary/Development Account resources; no money attached to specific TDB or Conference decisions |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — the Working Party on the Programme Plan and Programme Performance reviews programme performance and tracks management responses to independent evaluations (evaluation-recommendation follow-up), which is subprogramme/project-level, not per governing decision. The "Mandates of the Trade and Development Board" page under /about/evaluation is delegate-login-gated and evaluation-scoped, not a public decision tracker |
| 5 results-linked | no — Annual Evaluation Overviews and programme-performance reporting are at subprogramme/division/project level, not against specific decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Reform Tracker** — <https://unctad.org/topic/transport-and-trade-logistics/trade-facilitation/reform-tracker> — web tool for National Trade Facilitation Committees to manage WTO Trade Facilitation Agreement reforms; a member-State project-management tool, not a tracker of UNCTAD's own governing decisions.
- **Technical Cooperation Mandates** — <https://unctad.org/projects/mandates> — narrative page citing the legislative basis (Nairobi Maafikiano, TDB Decision 529) for technical cooperation; prose, not a register.
- **Evaluation at UNCTAD** — <https://unctad.org/about/evaluation> — Independent Evaluation Unit; subprogramme ("Reports mandated by the Board"), programme and project evaluation series with management responses; contact <evaluation@unctad.org>.

## Notes

- TDB verified (<https://unctad.org/about/trade-and-development-board>): UNCTAD's standing governing body between quadrennial Conferences; 160 members; annual, executive (up to 3×/yr) and special sessions; subsidiary bodies = Working Party on the Programme Plan and Programme Performance, two commissions (Investment/Enterprise/Development; Trade and Development), intergovernmental expert groups and expert meetings. President: Ambassador José R. Sánchez-Fung (Dominican Republic), elected 24 Nov 2025 at the 72nd session.
- UNCTAD is a permanent intergovernmental body established by the UN General Assembly (1964); it reports to the GA (through ECOSOC). The Conference sets the quadrennial mandate; the most recent, UNCTAD16 (Geneva, 20–23 Oct 2025), adopted the Spirit of Speightstown and the Bridgetown Covenant.
- General contact <unctadinfo@unctad.org>.
