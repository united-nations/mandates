# ECLAC — Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Accountability and evaluation** — <https://www.cepal.org/en/about/accountability-and-evaluation> — oversight and monitoring outcomes, audit reports, performance evaluations and Development Account project assessments, 2006–2026; grouped by year and project type, not by decision (verified).

## Notes

- **Dual nature.** ECLAC is an ECOSOC subsidiary organ meeting in session
  and a Secretariat entity headed by an Executive Secretary. Its governing
  bodies are two, and ECLAC says so itself: intergovernmental decisions are
  taken "by the member States at the session of the Commission **or the
  Committee of the Whole**" (verified,
  <https://www.cepal.org/en/about/strategy-and-legal-framework>).
- **Session of the Commission** — biennial, even years (40th: Lima,
  9–11 October 2024; 39th: 2022; 38th: 2020). Site:
  <https://periododesesiones.cepal.org>.
- **Committee of the Whole** — meets in the alternate years and adopts
  resolutions in its own right, in a parallel `(PLEN.nn)` numbering series;
  its 38th session (28 October 2025) approved the programme of work for
  2027, its 37th (2023) that for 2025. This is the body that carries the
  programme-of-work mandate in off-years, so it is listed as a governing
  body, not as a subsidiary organ.
- ECLAC has eight **subsidiary bodies** (Caribbean Development and
  Cooperation Committee; Regional Conference on Population and Development;
  Regional Conference on South-South Cooperation; Conference on Science,
  Innovation and ICTs; Regional Conference on Social Development; Regional
  Conference on Women; Regional Council for Planning of ILPES; Statistical
  Conference of the Americas) and two standing intergovernmental meetings
  (Ministerial Conference on the Information Society; Regional
  Intergovernmental Conference on Ageing). They hold their own numbered
  sessions and adopt their own agreements, and report to the Commission —
  not listed this round; see Open questions.
- `repository.eclac.org` 301-redirects to `repositorio.cepal.org`; the
  Spanish host is canonical.

## Open questions

- Whether ECLAC's subsidiary bodies belong in the governing-body list. They
  adopt outcome documents and biennial work programmes of their own, which
  meets the "decides in its own right" test, but those mandates are addressed
  to the subsidiary machinery rather than to ECLAC as an entity. Left out
  pending an explicit inclusion rule.
- Whether any per-resolution follow-up reporting exists in Spanish-only
  session documentation; searches in both languages found none, but ECLAC's
  session documents are not indexed as thoroughly as ESCAP's.
- Point 6 interoperability (deferred): the repository is DSpace and should
  expose OAI-PMH/REST.
