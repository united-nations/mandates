# WTO — World Trade Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Documents Online** (Documents Database) — <https://docs.wto.org/dol2fe/Pages/FE_Search/FE_S_S001.aspx>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — confirmed round 2; Documents Online is a document archive with no in-force/superseded status for decisions |
| 3 budget-linked | no — budget page publishes org-wide consolidated budgets and member contributions 2001–2025 with no link to the approving decisions (<https://www.wto.org/english/thewto_e/secre_e/budget_e.htm>, verified) |

| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

## Notes

- Documents Online: 310k documents, full-text search, EN/FR/ES, daily updates; structured Ministerial declarations list separate.
- Round 2: budget is CHF 204.9M for 2025; approval runs through the Committee on Budget, Finance and Administration and General Council, but only the resulting totals are surfaced on the budget page.
