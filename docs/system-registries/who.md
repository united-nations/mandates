# WHO — World Health Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Resolutions Portal** (Resolutions Database) — <https://resolutionsportal.who.int/>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — round 2 confirmed: Resolutions Portal has no active/superseded/in-force status field (chronological archive with topical metadata only) |
| 3 budget-linked | partial — WHO costs specific resolutions/decisions in its programme budget: PB Digital Platform "List of resolutions and decisions" page (2024–25: 29 resolutions + 23 decisions ≈ US$1.75bn base; 2026–27 mandates ≥ US$2.3bn) with PDF explainer; costings underlie Table 1/Fig. 9 of the PB document, not a per-resolution database, and not linked from the Resolutions Portal |
| 4 implementation-linked | no evidence in round 1 |
| 5 results-linked | partial — consolidated WHA "Progress reports" documents (e.g. A78/34) report progress against specific named resolutions each Assembly; a document series covering only resolutions that requested periodic reporting, not a tracking system |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **open.who.int** — Programme Budget Web Portal: quarterly-updated budget, financing, contributor and implementation data organized by GPW results hierarchy (outcomes/outputs), not by resolutions.
- **Programme Budget Digital Platform** — <https://www.who.int/about/accountability/budget/programme-budget-digital-platform-2026-2027> (and 2024–2025 edition): Executive Summary / Outcomes / Outputs / Dashboards / Explainers, including the resolutions-costing explainer page.

## Notes

- The AKN4UN "mandate-tracking tool" (EB154/33 para 26) is internal agenda management, not public.
- Public reform-specific trackers exist (SIP actions, consolidated-platform recommendations) — scoped to governance reform, not all resolutions.
- The resolutions-costing exercise is the strongest budget↔mandate link found in the specialized agencies, but it is a PB-document annex/explainer, not a queryable system; per-resolution amounts sit in the PB document tables/PDF, not on the portal page (verified 2026-07-18: the 2024–25 explainer page itself shows only aggregates).
