# IARC — International Agency for Research on Cancer

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

IARC is WHO's cancer agency, based in Lyon, established by World Health
Assembly resolution WHA18.44 (1965) under Article 18 of the WHO
Constitution. It is not a WHO department: it has its own Statute, its own
30 Participating States (Appendix 1 of the Statute, as at 6 May 2025), its
own finances, and its own governing body. It reports $0.36bn of expenses to
CEB (2011–2024).

## Governing bodies

- **Governing Council** — one representative of each Participating State
  plus the Director-General of WHO; meets in ordinary session at least once
  a year, normally around the World Health Assembly. Statute Article V is
  explicit about what it adopts: the budget, the financial regulations,
  control of expenditure, the size of the Secretariat; and, after
  considering the recommendations of the Scientific Council, the programme
  of permanent activities, any special project and any supplementary
  programme. It also elects the Director. Decisions are issued as numbered
  resolutions (`GC68_R01`…`GC68_R22` at the 2026 session).
  <https://governance.iarc.who.int/governing-councils/>

The **Scientific Council** (experts appointed in a personal capacity, not
State representatives) evaluates the Agency's activities and *recommends*
programmes; the Governing Council decides. It adopts no mandates of its own
and is therefore not listed as a governing body, although the Director
answers its recommendations in a standing document series.

The World Health Assembly is likewise not listed. Its role is residual but
real: Statute Article X requires WHA acceptance for amendments to the
Statute, and Rule 8(a) of the Rules of Procedure lets the Health Assembly
or the WHO Executive Board prescribe items for the Governing Council's
agenda. Neither amounts to adopting IARC's programme or budget.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — the governance site links every Governing Council and Scientific Council session to an Indico event holding the full document set, including the adopted resolutions: a consolidated `GC67-Resolutions-English.pdf` for 2025 and 22 individually numbered resolution PDFs for 2026 (e.g. `GC68_R12_IARC_MTS_2026_2030.pdf`), each in English and French. Documents from March 2009 onward are freely accessible; earlier sessions require a username and password issued on registration. Weak form: a per-session Indico archive with generic full-site search, not a resolutions database — borderline yes, scored yes for consistency with UNAIDS and UNDP |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — nothing anywhere distinguishes in-force from superseded or completed resolutions; the archive is chronological |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the Governing Council adopts the biennial Proposed Programme and Budget as a single document (GC67_8_PPB_2026-2027) by resolution, and the Annual Financial Report is presented each year, but that is org-wide budgeting approved *by* a resolution, not budget information attached *to* individual mandates; the scheme excludes org-wide budget transparency |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — the closest artefact is the standing "Director's response" series (GC67_4 to SC61, GC68_6 to SC62), which answers **Scientific Council recommendations**, not Governing Council resolutions; no follow-up or status document per GC resolution was found |
| 5 results-linked | no — scientific output is reported through the Biennial Report and the Medium-Term Strategy evaluation (GC67_6, evaluation of MTS 2021–2025), organized by branch and strategic objective, not against named resolutions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

None found. IARC publishes no budget, results or transparency portal
separate from its governing-body document series; financial information
reaches the public only through the Annual Financial Report tabled at each
Governing Council session.

## Notes

- The Statute, Rules of Procedure and Financial Regulations are published
  as one consolidated PDF, which also carries the current list of
  Participating States: <https://governance.iarc.who.int/documentation/statute-iarc.pdf>
  (verified; 14th edition, Appendix 1 as at 6 May 2025, 30 States).
- Session cadence verified from the agency's own governance pages:
  Governing Council 67th session 6–8 May 2025, 68th session 27–28 May 2026,
  69th session 2–4 June 2027; Scientific Council 62nd session 11–13
  February 2026.
- IARC's Indico instance (`events.iarc.who.int`, Indico v3.3.12) exposes
  per-event ICS export and site-wide search — relevant to point 6.
- No governance-secretariat email is published. The only public addresses
  are the general contact page and the Communications Officer
  (`terrassev@iarc.who.int`); the site otherwise gives the Lyon postal
  address and +33 (0)4 72 73 84 85.
