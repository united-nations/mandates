# UPU — Universal Postal Union

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Governing bodies

Bodies that adopt UPU's own mandates
(<https://www.upu.int/en/universal-postal-union/about-upu/bodies>, all
re-verified 2026-08-13):

- **Congress** — "the supreme authority of the Universal Postal Union",
  every four years; plenipotentiaries of 192 member countries adopt the Acts
  (Constitution, General Regulations, Convention, Postal Payment Services
  Agreement), the Union's four-year Strategy and the Congress decisions
  and resolutions. Mandate-adopting: **yes**. Latest: 28th Congress, Dubai,
  September 2025.
- **Council of Administration** (41 member countries, annual sessions in
  Berne) — "ensures the continuity of the UPU's work between Congresses,
  supervises its activities"; **approves the UPU's biennial budget and
  accounts as well as yearly updates**, may approve POC proposals for
  regulations or new procedures until the next Congress, and may take urgent
  measures. After each session it sends member countries a summary record
  plus **its resolutions and decisions**. Mandate-adopting: **yes**.
- **Postal Operations Council** (48 member countries) — "the technical and
  operational mind of the UPU"; handles operational, economic and commercial
  matters, adopts amendments to the Regulations between Congresses and makes
  recommendations to member countries on standards. Mandate-adopting:
  **yes**, in the narrower technical/regulatory sense.

**Excluded:** the **Consultative Committee** represents the wider postal
sector (NGOs, operators, suppliers) and is advisory — it takes no decisions
binding on the Union.

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

None. Closest candidate, deliberately not listed: the **Finance** page
(<https://www.upu.int/en/Universal-Postal-Union/About-UPU/Finance>, latest
items 15 December 2025 — Financial Statements 2024, Programme and Budget
2026) — a document page, not a portal, with no per-decision linkage. It does
document the governance chain: the Programme and Budget "stems from the
strategy of the Union, which gets adopted at UPU Congress", and the annual
budget "is approved by the Council of Administration".

## Notes

- Chronological PDF compilations only; no search interface anywhere.
- Nuance on "active": the Acts themselves (Constitution, Convention, General
  Regulations) are published as consolidated in-force versions as last
  amended by Congress, with update dates and circular amendments — but that
  covers treaty texts, not the decisions/resolutions corpus. Verified again
  2026-08-13: no explicit in-force badges on the decision compilations.
- **Access asymmetry found this round:** the Congress page routes session
  documents to the **UPU Documentation Centre, which requires member-country
  credentials**. Public access to the corpus runs only through the Acts page.
  CA and POC resolutions/decisions are circulated to member countries and
  designated operators rather than published as a public series.
