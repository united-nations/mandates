# UPU — Universal Postal Union

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Notes

- Chronological PDF compilations (Acts since 1947, Compendium of Congress decisions 1947–2016); no search interface.
- Nuance on "active": the Acts themselves (Constitution, Convention, General Regulations) are published as consolidated in-force versions as last amended by Congress — but that covers treaty texts, not the decisions/resolutions corpus.
