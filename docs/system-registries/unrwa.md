# UNRWA — United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. No centralized system compiling UNRWA's governing-body decisions. The Advisory Commission "Key Documents" page (<https://www.unrwa.org/who-we-are/advisory-commission/key-documents>) is a curated governance page, not a compilation of a decision corpus and with no in-force/superseded status.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | no — no compiled, findable collection of UNRWA's governing-body decisions. The mandate-renewing GA resolutions live in the UN's own document systems (ODS / mandates.un.org), not a UNRWA-maintained compilation; the Advisory Commission is advisory and adopts no binding resolutions/decisions, so there is no decision series to compile. The Key Documents page holds foundational texts plus a long run of Commissioner-General **statements to** the Commission — one-directional reporting, not adopted decisions |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded tracking; UNRWA's mandate is renewed en bloc by periodic GA resolution (**now through 30 June 2029** — see below), tracked nowhere as a structured registry |
| 3 budget-linked | no — budget/expenditure published by Core Programme / Emergency Appeal / project and donor (Programme Budget "Blue Book", open.unrwa.org IATI portal), with no linkage to specific GA resolutions or Advisory Commission conclusions |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — implementation reported programmatically (annual Commissioner-General report to the GA, results framework), not against specific decisions |
| 5 results-linked | no — outcomes reported at programme/strategic level, not per decision |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Governing bodies — re-verified, unchanged

All three grounds for keeping the **General Assembly** as UNRWA's
governing body were checked adversarially in round 3 and all three hold:

1. **No executive board.** Confirmed. UNRWA has no board; the
   Commissioner-General reports directly to the General Assembly.
2. **The Advisory Commission is advisory only.** Confirmed. It "advises
   and assists" the Commissioner-General, is *consulted* on the budget,
   and holds no decision-making or voting authority. Its published output
   is statements, an annual report and procedural texts (Rules of
   Procedure, Sub-Committee ToR) — no series of adopted decisions.
   Composition as of round 2 stands: 29 members + 4 observers (EU, League
   of Arab States, OIC, Palestine).
3. **The GA renews the mandate and approves the budget.** Confirmed on
   both counts. UNRWA's own wording: *"The UNRWA Programme Budget (General
   Fund), approved by the General Assembly …"*; the Commissioner-General
   submits the programme budget to the General Assembly **after
   consultation with** the Advisory Commission. UNRWA also has its own UN
   programme-budget section ("Palestine refugees"), so the GA appropriates
   for it directly as well as approving the voluntarily-funded General
   Fund.

**Mandate expiry corrected.** The recorded "30 June 2026" is out of date.
**A/RES/80/78, "Assistance to Palestine refugees", adopted 5 December 2025**
(55th plenary meeting; issued 8 December 2025; on the report of the Fourth
Committee, A/80/537 para. 15) states at operative paragraph 6:

> "Decides to extend the mandate of the Agency until 30 June 2029, without
> prejudice to the provisions of paragraph 11 of General Assembly
> resolution 194 (III)."

Verified against the official PDF of A/RES/80/78 retrieved from the UN
document system. Adopted 151–10–14. **New term: 1 July 2026 – 30 June 2029**
(a three-year renewal, up from the previous three-year cycle ending
30 June 2026).

## Notes

- Round 3 re-check of the Key Documents page (via the 20 May 2026 archive
  snapshot; the live site is Cloudflare-gated) shows it is **longer than
  round 2 described** but not different in kind. It carries three groups:
  *Historical Documents* (GA res 302(IV), A/77/314, and roughly a dozen GA
  resolutions extending Advisory Commission membership to individual
  States and to the OIC as observer), *Organizational and Procedural*
  (Ad Hoc Working Group outcome documents, edited AdCom Rules of Procedure
  + Sub-Committee ToR, rotation scenario), and a chronological run of
  *Commissioner-General's Speeches* to the Commission covering roughly
  2010–2025 (latest: 19 November 2025). The corrected characterization is
  "a curated governance page", not "a short curated list" — but the
  finding is unchanged: it contains no adopted-decision corpus and no
  status field, so `documents` stays `false`.
- The page is also **stale on the headline fact**: it still lists a "GA
  Resolution on the extension of the Mandate of the Agency until 2023" and
  carries nothing for A/RES/80/78.
- The Advisory Commission section includes a **"Members Area"** — a gated
  sub-site for Commission members. Whatever AdCom working documents exist
  there are not public and cannot be assessed.
- Because the operative mandates are GA resolutions, they are already
  covered by the UN Secretariat / mandates.un.org side of this survey;
  UNRWA itself maintains no decision registry.
- Site remains Cloudflare-protected and 403s every non-browser client
  *and* a headless browser (the challenge does not clear headlessly).
  Round-3 page checks therefore rest on Wayback snapshots dated 20 May
  2026 (status 200 for who-we-are, advisory-commission, key-documents and
  funding-trends), plus primary UN document sources for the resolution.
- Scores 0 of 5 — like UNESCO, an acknowledged gap: no decision-tracking
  system.
