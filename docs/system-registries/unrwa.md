# UNRWA — United Nations Relief and Works Agency for Palestine Refugees in the Near East

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. No centralized system compiling UNRWA's governing-body decisions. The Advisory Commission "Key Documents" page (<https://www.unrwa.org/who-we-are/advisory-commission/key-documents>) is a short curated list of foundational/historical documents (GA res 302(IV), Rules of Procedure, GA resolutions on Advisory Commission membership, host-country agreements), not a compilation of the decision corpus and with no in-force/superseded status.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | no — no compiled, findable collection of UNRWA's governing-body decisions. The mandate-renewing GA resolutions live in the UN's own document systems (ODS / mandates.un.org), not a UNRWA-maintained compilation; the Advisory Commission is advisory and adopts no binding resolutions/decisions (it issues statements and an annual report), so there is no decision series to compile. The curated Key Documents page holds only a handful of foundational texts |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded tracking; UNRWA's mandate is renewed en bloc by periodic GA resolution (currently through 30 June 2026), tracked nowhere as a structured registry |
| 3 budget-linked | no — budget/expenditure published by Core Programme / Emergency Appeal / project and donor (Programme Budget "Blue Book", open.unrwa.org IATI portal), with no linkage to specific GA resolutions or Advisory Commission conclusions |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — implementation reported programmatically (annual Commissioner-General report to the GA, results framework), not against specific decisions |
| 5 results-linked | no — outcomes reported at programme/strategic level, not per decision |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNRWA Transparency Portal (open.unrwa.org)** — <https://open.unrwa.org/> — IATI-based budget, contribution and expenditure data (UNRWA IATI identifier XM-DAC-41130), organized by Core Programmes, Emergency Appeals, projects and donors; not linked to governing-body decisions.
- **Funding Trends / Donor Charts** — <https://www.unrwa.org/how-you-can-help/government-partners/funding-trends> — contribution rankings and trends.

## Notes

- Governing structure is unusual: UNRWA has no executive board. It was established by, and its mandate is periodically renewed by, the **General Assembly** (res 302(IV), 8 Dec 1949). The **Advisory Commission** (29 members + 4 observers — EU, League of Arab States, OIC, Palestine — as of July 2025; Brazil chair 2025–2026) "advises and assists" the Commissioner-General but holds no decision-making/voting authority, so it produces statements and reports rather than a corpus of adopted decisions.
- Because the operative mandates are GA resolutions, they are already covered by the UN Secretariat / mandates.un.org side of this survey; UNRWA itself maintains no decision registry.
- Site is Cloudflare-protected and 403s non-browser clients; Key Documents page structure and Advisory Commission composition verified via search-indexed UNRWA pages and the July 2025 members/observers PDF.
- Public contact: <https://www.unrwa.org/contact-us> (media/public information: hq-pio@unrwa.org).
- Scores 0 of 5 — like UNESCO, an acknowledged gap: no decision-tracking system.
