# IOM — International Organization for Migration

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Council Resolutions Database** (Resolutions Database) — <https://governingbodies.iom.int/council-resolutions>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — confirmed round 2; resolutions database has category filters only, no active/superseded status metadata |
| 3 budget-linked | no — Programme and Budget (C/xxx/6) is adopted/revised by Council and SCPF resolutions (e.g. S/36/RES/40 revising the 2025 P&B), but budget figures are programme/project-level; no system links resolutions to resourcing |
| 4 implementation-linked | no evidence in round 1 |
| 5 results-linked | no — financial reports and Director General reporting go to SCPF/Council as session documents; no reporting against specific resolutions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- SCPF (Standing Committee on Programmes and Finance) pages on the same governing-bodies site: reports (<https://governingbodies.iom.int/scpf-reports>) and SCPF resolutions list — budget/financial oversight documents per session.
- Programme and Budget and Financial Report documents per Council/SCPF session on governingbodies.iom.int.
