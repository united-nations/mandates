# UNCDF — United Nations Capital Development Fund

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Executive Board Decision Tracking Tables** (Decision Tracking) — <https://www.undp.org/executive-board/decisions-of-the-board> — run by UNDP for the joint UNDP/UNFPA/UNOPS Executive Board; UNCDF-directed requests are tracked inside it as a named responsible entity.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — decisions of the joint Executive Board, including those addressed to UNCDF, are compiled and publicly findable |
| 2 mandates (current vs replaced) | no — per-request status only (Completed / Ongoing / In Progress); no current-vs-replaced registry of decisions |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the tracking tables carry no financial fields; verified directly on the 2025 and 2024 PDFs (zero occurrences of `$`, `USD` or `million`) |
| 4 implementation-linked | yes — UNCDF appears as the responsible entity against specific decision paragraphs, each with a deadline, a UNCDF-specific progress entry and a status |
| 5 results-linked | no — the progress column records follow-up actions, not outcomes; results reporting is Strategic-Framework-based |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNCDF Transparency Window and Data Portal** — <https://transparency.uncdf.org/> — live (re-checked 2026-08-13); contributions and expenses 2016–2024, dashboards 2020–2024, broken down by donor type (bilateral 54%, EU 18%, UN development system 23%, private 5%) and by 41 LDCs across four regions; 2024 contributions $112M, expenses $82.9M. Organized by donor and geography, not by Executive Board decisions.

## Notes

- **Round 3 raises UNCDF from 0 to 2 (documents + implementation).** Round 2 recorded "no system of its own" and stopped there; that was right about ownership and wrong about coverage. Reading the actual PDFs shows UNCDF-directed requests are tracked exactly as UNDP's own are:
  - *2025 table* (decision 2025/1, paras 4, 5 and 6): responsible division "BMS, UNCDF", deadlines including one written specifically for UNCDF ("Second Regular Session 2025 / First Regular Session 2025 for UNCDF"), UNCDF-specific progress narratives ("During 2025, UNCDF is preparing its Strategic Framework 2026-2029 …"), status "In Progress".
  - *2024 table*: an entire decision block on the midterm review of the UNCDF Strategic Framework 2022–2025 and the report on results achieved by UNCDF in 2023 (DP/2024/22), responsible division "UNCDF" alone, with per-paragraph deadlines, UNCDF progress narratives and statuses "Completed", "Completed and ongoing", "In Progress"; plus an audit row with deadline "Second Regular Session 2024" and status "Completed".
- **The rule applied** (offered for the inclusion-rule note): *an entity earns points from a registry another organization runs only where the registry tracks decisions addressed to that entity individually — the entity is named as the responsible party and carries its own deadline, progress entry and status — not merely mentioned inside a request addressed to others.*
- That rule is consistent with the parallel UNV finding (UNV appears as responsible division against decision 2024/17 paras 3 and 6, with deadlines, progress and status) and it also **confirms the earlier negative finding for UNOPS**, this time on positive evidence: in the 2025 table every UNOPS-directed paragraph (e.g. decision 2025/1 paras 9 and 10 on hedging) carries responsible division "N/A" and status "N/A". UNDP's table is a UNDP-family table — UNDP, UNCDF and UNV are tracked, UNOPS is not. UNFPA's own table is explicitly scoped to "requests **to UNFPA**" and does not track UNCDF either.
- UNCDF is governed by the Executive Board of UNDP, UNFPA and UNOPS; it has no governing body of its own. See the UNDP dossier for the shared machinery.
- A 2026 Publish What You Fund review notes UNCDF publishes to IATI but with no transaction data.
- The General Assembly created UNCDF (res 2186 (XXI)) and sets system-wide policy through the QCPR, but does not govern its programme or appropriate its budget: UNCDF has no UN regular-budget section — it does not appear anywhere in the PPB 2027 entity list. The round-2 removal of the GA is **correct**.
