## Notes

- Round 3 read the tables themselves rather than inferring from siblings. undp.org still returns 403 to every non-interactive client; the page and the PDFs were retrieved through a headless browser session.
- **Coverage corrected: 2016–2025, not 2018–2025.** Ten annual tables are linked, described on the page as "the status of Executive Board decisions related to UNDP (including joint decisions)".
- **Staleness finding.** The three newest tables (2023, 2024, 2025) all carry the June 2025 refresh stamp and no 2026 table has appeared. As of August 2026 the tracking is fourteen months old — the least current of the four joint-board/UNICEF systems in this batch (UNICEF refreshed 2026-06-12, UNFPA May 2026).
- Budget = false now rests on **direct evidence** rather than round 2's genre inference: the 2025 PDF was extracted in full and has no financial column and no monetary values anywhere.
- Status vocabulary observed across the 2025 table: N/A (38, used where the paragraph asks nothing of UNDP), In Progress (16), Ongoing (7), Completed (4).
- The table is a **UNDP-family** table, not a board-wide one. UNCDF and UNV are tracked inside it as named responsible entities with their own deadlines, progress entries and statuses; UNOPS is not — every UNOPS-directed paragraph carries responsible division "N/A" and status "N/A". See the UNCDF dossier for the rule this establishes.
- Covers the joint UNDP/UNFPA/UNOPS Executive Board.

## Governing bodies

- **Executive Board (UNDP, UNFPA and UNOPS)** — the sole governing body; approves the strategic plan and the integrated budget.
- **General Assembly / ECOSOC — the round-2 removal is correct.** UNDP has no UN regular-budget section (it does not appear in the PPB 2027 entity list); it is wholly voluntarily funded and its budget is approved by the Executive Board. The GA created it (res 2029 (XX)) and sets system-wide policy through the QCPR, which is what the `note` now records.
