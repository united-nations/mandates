# UNICRI — United Nations Interregional Crime and Justice Research Institute

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNICRI is an autonomous research institute governed by a **Board of Trustees**. The Board's own decisions and session records are not published, but the Board's **annual report to ECOSOC** is: a complete symboled series (`E/CN.15/YYYY/9`, "Results achieved by UNICRI — Report of the Board of Trustees"), 2012–2024, in all six UN languages, on one page. This is the strongest public governance artifact of the four research-and-training institutes in this round — but it is a narrative results report approved *by* a Board decision, not a compilation *of* Board decisions, and it is a PDF series rather than a system. Not scored, mirroring UNITAR and UNU.

## Notes

- Governing body: the **Board of Trustees** — statute art. IV. **Seven elected members** serving in individual capacity for five-year terms (renewable once), selected by the **Commission on Crime Prevention and Criminal Justice** on nomination by the Secretary-General and endorsed by **ECOSOC**, on the basis of equitable geographical and gender distribution; plus **four ex officio members** (a representative of the Secretary-General, a representative of the UNDP Administrator, a representative of the host country Italy, and the UNICRI Director). Current officers: President Carolina Lizárraga Houghton (Peru), Vice-President Jonathan Lucas (Seychelles), Rapporteur Omar Rifai (Jordan) (<https://unicri.org/governing-body>, verified).
- Functions (statute art. IV.3): formulate principles, policies and guidelines; consider and approve the work programme and budget proposals; evaluate completed and ongoing activities; make recommendations; report periodically to ECOSOC through the Crime Commission. The Board **"shall meet at least once every two years"** (art. IV.4) — in practice it meets roughly annually (2024 meeting: 22–23 October).
- The Board operates "under the overall guidance of the Committee on Crime Prevention and Control" (art. IV.1) — the predecessor of today's Commission on Crime Prevention and Criminal Justice. The Crime Commission elects the Board's members and receives its reports; it is a supervisory/constitutive channel, not the body that adopts UNICRI's programme of work (see UNCERTAINTIES).
- Established under ECOSOC resolution 1086 B (XXXIX) of 1965 (operational 1968); presently governed by the statute adopted by **ECOSOC resolution 1989/56 of 24 May 1989** (<https://unicri.org/sites/default/files/2019-10/UNICRI%20Statute.pdf>, verified; scanned PDF, text extracted). Note the statute names Rome as the headquarters (art. III.3); the Institute has in fact been headquartered in Turin for decades.
- Entirely voluntarily funded — "does not receive any funding from the regular budget of the United Nations".
- Contact: no Board-secretariat contact is published. General: <mailto:unicri.publicinfo@un.org>, +39 011 6537 111, Viale Maestri del Lavoro 10, 10127 Turin, Italy (<https://unicri.org/contactus>, verified). Liaison offices in Rome, New York, Geneva and Brussels each have their own address; `unicri.org/contacts` returns 403 — the working path is `/contactus`.
