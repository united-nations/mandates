# UNAIDS — Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

UNAIDS is a cosponsored joint programme. Its governing body is the
**Programme Coordinating Board (PCB)** — 22 government members, the 11
Cosponsors and 5 NGO representatives — which reports to ECOSOC. The PCB
meets twice a year (June/December) and adopts numbered decision points at
each session.

## Systems

- **PCB Meeting Archive / PCB Decisions** (Meeting archive) — <https://www.unaids.org/en/whoweare/pcb>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — each PCB session publishes a "decisions, recommendations and conclusions" document, and all agenda documents are posted per-meeting in the governance/meeting archive back to 1995; officially compiled by session and publicly findable. Weak form: a per-meeting PDF archive, **not** a queryable decisions database like UNODC's RDDB — borderline yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded registry of decision points; the archive is a chronological run of session documents |
| 3 budget-linked | no — financial data lives in the Results and Transparency Portal (open.unaids.org), organized by UBRAF results area, cosponsor and country, not by PCB decision |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — follow-up to PCB decisions is handled through UBRAF workplan/budget follow-up documents (organized by results area, e.g. PCB53 "Follow-up actions to the 2024–2025 Workplan and Budget") and ad-hoc papers responding to individual decision points; "timely follow-up to relevant PCB decisions" is itself a UBRAF performance indicator, but there is no systematic per-decision status matrix as a system (contrast UNDP's Decision/Request/Deadline/Progress/Status tracking tables) |
| 5 results-linked | no — the UBRAF Indicator Matrix and Performance Monitoring Reports report at output/indicator level against the strategy, not against specific PCB decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNAIDS Results and Transparency Portal** (a.k.a. Financial Dashboard) — <https://open.unaids.org/> — UBRAF 2022–2026 income, expenditure and results data by cosponsor, results area and country; org-wide open data, not linked to individual PCB decisions.

## Notes

- The task flagged a PCB "decision-monitoring / follow-up matrix" as a possible implementation/mandates point. What exists is (a) UBRAF workplan-and-budget follow-up documents structured by results area, and (b) per-request response papers prepared for specific decision points (e.g. DP 6.8 at PCB52, DP 9.3 at the Sept-2025 Bureau). Follow-up is real but is delivered through the results framework and one-off documents — not a queryable per-decision tracking system — so no implementation point is earned. Score = 1.
- No consolidated cross-meeting compilation/index of PCB decisions was found; decisions must be read from each session's page. This is why documents is a borderline rather than clean yes.
- open.unaids.org and the UBRAF portal return HTTP 403 to non-browser clients; characterization is from UNAIDS governance pages and PCB document metadata (both verified) plus prior knowledge of the UBRAF portal's structure.

## Open questions

- Whether any single PCB document is a true per-decision status matrix (a full read of the follow-up PDFs was blocked by compression/encoding); if one is, implementation could rise to 'partial' (PDF form).
- Whether open.unaids.org anywhere tags spending or results to specific PCB decision points (none evident) — would bear on points 3 and 5.
