# UNAIDS — Joint United Nations Programme on HIV/AIDS

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

UNAIDS is a cosponsored joint programme. Its governing body is the
**Programme Coordinating Board (PCB)** — 22 government members, the 11
Cosponsors and 5 NGO representatives — which reports to ECOSOC. The PCB
meets twice a year and adopts numbered decision points at each session.

## Systems

- **PCB Meeting Archive / PCB Decisions** (Meeting archive) — <https://www.unaids.org/en/aboutunaids/unaidsprogrammecoordinatingboard/pcbmeetingarchive> *(round 3: a dedicated archive URL, more precise than the PCB landing page previously recorded)*

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes (borderline) — each PCB session publishes a "decisions, recommendations and conclusions" document and all agenda documents are posted per meeting back to 1995. **Re-confirmed round 3 that this is a per-meeting PDF archive and nothing more**: there is no cross-meeting compilation, no index of decision points and no searchable decisions tool |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/replaced registry of decision points; the archive is a chronological run of session documents |
| 3 budget-linked | no — financial data lives in the Results and Transparency Portal (open.unaids.org, HTTP 200 round 3), organized by UBRAF results area, cosponsor and country, not by PCB decision |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — follow-up runs through UBRAF workplan/budget follow-up documents (organized by results area) and ad-hoc papers responding to individual decision points; "timely follow-up to relevant PCB decisions" is itself a UBRAF performance indicator, but there is no per-decision status matrix as a system (contrast UNDP's Decision/Request/Deadline/Progress/Status tables) |
| 5 results-linked | no — the UBRAF Indicator Matrix and Performance Monitoring Reports report at output/indicator level against the strategy, not against specific PCB decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)
