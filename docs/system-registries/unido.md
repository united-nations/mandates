# UNIDO — United Nations Industrial Development Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Governing bodies

Bodies that adopt UNIDO's own mandates:

- **General Conference** — <https://www.unido.org/resources/policymaking-organs/general-conference>
  — biennial supreme policymaking organ. Verified verbatim 2026-08-13: it
  "determines the guiding principles and policies of the Organization, and
  approves the budget and work programme of UNIDO", appoints the Director
  General every four years and elects the members of the Industrial
  Development Board and the Programme and Budget Committee.
  Mandate-adopting: **yes** (resolutions and decisions, e.g. GC.21/INF/4).
  Latest session: 21st, Riyadh, 23–27 November 2025; next: 22nd, 22–26
  November 2027.
- **Industrial Development Board** — <https://www.unido.org/resources/policymaking-organs/industrial-development-board>
  — reviews implementation of the work programme and the regular and
  operational budgets, recommends the programme and budget to the General
  Conference, and adopts its own decisions and conclusions addressed to the
  Director General (e.g. IDB.53/36 requesting publication of outcome
  indicators on COMPASS). Mandate-adopting: **yes**.

**Excluded:** the **Programme and Budget Committee** is UNIDO's third
"policymaking organ" by name, but it is a subsidiary of the Board that
examines the programme and budget and *makes recommendations* to it — it
does not adopt mandates. Flagged as a borderline call.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — one compilation document per session, six languages, sessions back to 1985 (texts online from the 7th session, 1997) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — per-session compilation PDFs only; no in-force/replaced distinction, no cross-session search |
| 3 budget-linked | no — budget/funding transparency lives in COMPASS at project level, not linked to GC/IDB decisions |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — no per-decision status reporting found; follow-up is by narrative DG reports per agenda item |
| 5 results-linked | no — results (outcomes/outputs) are reported per project and, since 2026, per MTPF results indicator in COMPASS/IATI, never against governing-body decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

Total unchanged: **1**.

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNIDO COMPASS** — <https://compass.unido.org/> — live (re-verified
  2026-08-13; `open.unido.org` still 301-redirects here). IATI-based
  transparency portal with per-project budgets, outcomes/outputs, donors and
  documents. **New since round 2:** COMPASS now also carries the results
  indicators of the integrated results and performance framework for the
  medium-term programme framework 2026–2029, published in response to an
  Industrial Development Board request (IDB.53/36; progress reported to the
  General Conference in GC.21/7). Still organized by project and results
  framework, not by decisions.
- Other UNIDO platforms (`stat.unido.org`, `iap.unido.org`,
  `hub.unido.org`) are statistical/knowledge products, not budget or results
  transparency; not listed.
