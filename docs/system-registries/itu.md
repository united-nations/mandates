# ITU — International Telecommunication Union

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Council Resolutions & Decisions** (Resolutions in Force) — <https://www.itu.int/pub/S-CONF-CL>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | yes |
| 3 budget-linked | no — in-force compilation includes budget resolutions as documents but carries no financial data; Council reporting mentions budget only at aggregate level |
| 4 implementation-linked | no evidence in round 1 |
| 5 results-linked | no — annual report to Council (per PP Res 71) includes a "Status of Implementation of PP Resolutions" section, but that is implementation status inside a document, not results reporting against resolutions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **ITU Council snapshot report** — <https://www.itu.int/reports/council/> — session outcomes, strategic-plan progress, aggregate budget notes (verified).
- **Annual activities report** (per PP Res 71) — <https://council.itu.int/2024/en/networking/resources/annual-activities-report/> — strategic-plan/activities reporting with Power BI dashboards on Connect 2030 targets (verified).

## Notes

- Annual consolidated Council resolutions/decisions in force (free PDF); PP Final Acts partly behind free registration/TIES.
- The 2023 annual report references a "web-based platform reporting on implementation of PP Decisions and Resolutions" (itu.int/net4/Search/CL24/Main/Reader), but that URL returned HTTP 500 in round 2 — likely session-bound; could not confirm per-resolution granularity.
