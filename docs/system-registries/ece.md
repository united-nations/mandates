# ECE — Economic Commission for Europe

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNECE publishes no compilation of Commission or Executive
  Committee decisions. Adopted decisions exist only as lettered items —
  A (71), B (71), … — whose texts sit in Chapter IV of the 50-page biennial
  report (E/ECE/1512, verified), reached through the per-session
  documentation tab rather than through any decisions list.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | no — no decisions register, decisions page or searchable index. Draft decisions circulate as session documents (E/ECE/1516, E/ECE/2025/L.1, E/ECE/2025/L.2) and the adopted texts appear inside the biennial report; the session pages list documents per agenda item, not decisions per se. Arguably `partial`; see Open questions |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded status for decisions anywhere |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the ECE programme budget goes to EXCOM as a whole-of-Commission document (e.g. "ECE PPB 2024-Part II" to EXCOM's 126th meeting) and is organized by subprogramme; Open UNECE publishes 2026 regular-budget resources against deliverables and the organizational chart, again with no decision linkage |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — E/ECE/1515 is a *progress report on the work of the Commission* on the biennial theme, and E/ECE/1514 a *report on activities of the Executive Committee*; both are programme-level. EXCOM "examines with the Chairpersons of the Sectoral Committees their report on the implementation of their programme of work" — again programme, not decision |
| 5 results-linked | no — the biennial report and the RSRS Strategic Results Framework report at subprogramme/outcome level, never against a specific Commission decision |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Open UNECE** — <https://unece.org/open-unece-1> — "All information on the
  governance, implementation and reporting of UNECE resources": Governance
  Framework, Programme Planning and Reporting, Evaluation, Audit, Project
  Management, Resource Mobilization. Organized by subprogramme and project,
  never by decision (verified).
- **RSRS repository** — <https://unece.org/excom/RSRS> — documents of the
  "right-sizing and right-servicing the subsidiary architecture" initiative,
  including issue papers per subprogramme cluster, a Strategic Results
  Framework and a 2026 prioritization exercise. A live reform process about
  the mandate stock, but not a register of it (verified, current to June 2026).

## Notes

- **Dual nature.** ECE is both an ECOSOC subsidiary organ and a Secretariat
  entity headed by an Executive Secretary. Its governing body is **the
  Commission**, "the highest governing body of the organization", whose
  sessions are held **every two years** at the Palais des Nations
  (verified, <https://unece.org/commission-sessions-1>). Latest: seventy-first
  session, 9–10 April 2025.
- **EXCOM is a genuine second governing body.** "In between the biennial
  sessions of the Commission, the Executive Committee acts on behalf of the
  Commission and is competent on all matters related to ECE activities…
  reviews, evaluates and approves in due time the Programme of work of the
  sectoral committees… approves the set-up, renewal, discontinuance, terms of
  reference and work plans of groups under the Sectoral Committees"
  (verified, <https://unece.org/executive-committee-excom-0>). It therefore
  approves ECE's programme of work and is listed.
- **The eight sectoral committees also decide in their own right**: "the
  programme of work of each subprogramme is approved by the Sectoral
  Committee and, subsequently, by the EXCOM." They are Environmental Policy;
  Inland Transport; Conference of European Statisticians; Sustainable Energy;
  Steering Committee on Trade Capacity and Standards; Forests and the Forest
  Industry; Urban Development, Housing and Land Management; Innovation,
  Competitiveness and Public-Private Partnerships (roster verified as of
  9 June 2026, <https://unece.org/governing-bodies>). Listed as one overview
  entry rather than eight rows.
- ECE is the only regional commission of the five that does **not** adopt
  numbered resolutions in a continuing series; it adopts per-session lettered
  decisions. That, more than any tooling gap, is why nothing here is
  compilable in the way ESCAP's 717 resolutions or ESCWA's 336 are.
- unece.org returns HTTP 403 to non-browser clients and serves a Cloudflare
  challenge; every URL above was rendered in a real browser. E/ECE/1512 was
  read through `documents.un.org` because unece.org blocks direct PDF fetches.

## Open questions

- The `documents` call is the closest borderline in this batch. Decision
  texts *are* public and permanently archived — inside a report. A looser
  reading (as applied to UNAIDS/UNITAR/UNCCD per-session decision PDFs in the
  gap-fill round) would give `partial`; scored `no` here because there is no
  artefact anywhere that presents ECE decisions as a set.
- Whether EXCOM meeting conclusions (per-meeting PDFs under
  <https://unece.org/meetings-6>) amount to a decisions series worth
  scoring — not inspected item by item this round.
- Whether the RSRS prioritization exercise produces a durable mandate
  inventory; if so it would be the first ECE artefact scorable on point 2.
