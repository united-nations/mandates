# IAEA — International Atomic Energy Agency

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **GC Resolutions Archive** (Resolutions Database) — <https://www.iaea.org/gc-archives/type/resolutions>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — re-verified round 3 in a browser (the site is Cloudflare-gated to non-browser clients, HTTP 403). Archive now runs to **GC70 (2026)**; three GC special sessions (1986, 2019, 2023); type facets Documents 9,099 · **Resolutions 4,086** · Records 2,331 · Agenda 214; two search boxes (by title, by symbol) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — re-confirmed round 3; the archive is a session/type document list with no status (current/replaced) metadata anywhere |
| 3 budget-linked | no — budget lives in the programme-level "Agency's Programme and Budget" document (e.g. GC(69)/6), adopted by a GC resolution but with no system linking resolutions to resourcing |
| 4 implementation-linked | no |
| 5 results-linked | no — the DG issues individual reports pursuant to specific GC resolutions as GC documents, but no system connects resolutions to outcome reporting |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Notes

- General Conference only; no Board of Governors documents public.
- Round 3 governance URLs: <https://www.iaea.org/about/governance> is live and now carries **dedicated sub-pages** — <https://www.iaea.org/about/governance/general-conference> and <https://www.iaea.org/about/governance/board-of-governors> — so the two governing-body entries no longer need to share one overview URL. The governance page states: "The IAEA's policy-making bodies decide on the Agency's programmes and budgets… the General Conference of all Member States and the 35-member Board of Governors."
- Round 3 contact correction: the recorded contact email `MTCD-CSS-Exhibitions.Contact-Point@iaea.org` was traced to its source — it is the **Exhibitions** paragraph of the General Conference page ("Member States and accredited organizations wishing to take part as exhibitors… please contact the IAEA Conference Services Section"). It is a side-event/exhibition contact, not a governing-body contact. The correct governing-body secretariat is the **Secretariat of Policy-Making Organs** (<https://www.iaea.org/about/organizational-structure/offices-reporting-to-the-director-general/secretariat-of-policy-making-organs>, verified live): "to enable the Policy-Making Organs — the General Conference and the Board of Governors — to effectively perform their statutory responsibilities". That page publishes no email; the Agency's general contact page is <https://www.iaea.org/contact> (footer: Vienna International Centre, PO Box 100, A-1400 Vienna; +43 (1) 2600-0).
