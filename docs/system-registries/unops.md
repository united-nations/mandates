# UNOPS — United Nations Office for Project Services

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Executive Board Documents** (Document Repository) — <https://www.unops.org/about/governance/executive-board/executive-board-documents>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — repository of "all UNOPS-related Annual Reports, document sessions, and Executive Board decisions, reports and statements from 1994-present", filterable by document type and year |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — a document archive only; no in-force/superseded status for decisions |
| 3 budget-linked | no — no financial amounts tied to specific decisions |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — no decision-tracking table for UNOPS. The joint-board tracking tables track only their own agency's requests: UNDP's covers "requests to UNDP", UNFPA's "specific and time-bound requests to UNFPA"; neither carries a UNOPS column. (UNOPS's separate oversight-recommendation monitoring, e.g. "78% of management targets met", tracks audit recommendations, not EB decisions.) |
| 5 results-linked | no — no outcome reporting against specific decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNOPS Data Portal** — <https://data.unops.org/> — IATI-based project and expenditure data with maps and infographics; UNOPS joined IATI in 2011 as the first UN body to publish in the format. Organized by project/country/sector/donor, not by Board decisions. (WebFetch returned only the page title — a client-rendered app; nature verified via UNOPS transparency page and IATI descriptions.)

## Notes

- Governing body is the shared **Executive Board of UNDP, UNFPA and UNOPS** (GA res 48/162, 1993; 36 Member States, elected annually through ECOSOC for three-year terms). Reuses the existing `undp-board` ref in `un-system.json`.
- UNOPS decisions are adopted at the joint Board's UNOPS-segment items and numbered jointly (e.g. decision 2025/6 on liquid excess reserves; DP/OPS/… series). They are compiled in the annual joint compilation of decisions and on the UNOPS EB documents page, but no decision-tracking/implementation matrix exists for the UNOPS segment — unlike UNDP and UNFPA, which each publish their own (agency-specific) tracking tables.
- Round 2 (gap-fill): confirmed via UNFPA 2024 tracking table (26 pp, "requests to UNFPA"; UNOPS appears only inside joint "UNDP, UNFPA and UNOPS" decision references) and the UNOPS governance/EB-documents pages (no tracking tooling described).
