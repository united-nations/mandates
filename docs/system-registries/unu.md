# UNU — United Nations University

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNU is a research institute with a light governing body (the UNU Council). No centralized decision-tracking or mandate-registry system exists, and the Council's session records/decisions are not published as a public series. Only chronological annual reports are available.

## Governing bodies — re-verified, removal CORRECT

The **UNU Council** is the governing body, and the GA/ECOSOC removal is
**correct**. The decisive evidence is the Charter itself
(<https://unu.edu/about/unu-charter>, as amended 2009 and 2013, verified
live):

- Article IV para. 4(d): the Council shall "Consider and approve the work
  programme and adopt the budget of the University on the basis of
  proposals submitted to it by the Rector."
- Article IX para. 1: "Capital costs and recurrent costs of the University
  shall be met from **voluntary contributions**" (governments and
  non-governmental sources), with degree-programme costs also met from
  tuition fees (para. 2bis, 2009 amendment).
- Article IX para. 9 specifies that when UNU uses UN administrative
  services, "no extra cost to the regular budget of the United Nations is
  incurred."

So the General Assembly appropriates **nothing** for UNU — consistent with
UNU having no UN programme-budget section
(`data/references/ppb_sections_and_entities.tsv`) and no `this-site`
Mandate Registry entry. The GA/ECOSOC relationship is *reporting*
(the Council reports annually to both, and to the UNESCO Executive Board),
not governing. Established by GA resolution 3081 (XXVIII), 6 December
1973; headquarters Tokyo.

## Notes

- The Council's annual report to the General Assembly is issued in the UN
  document system, but UNU's own site does not surface Council decisions,
  agendas or session records as a browsable series — the closest public
  artifact is the yearly annual report. Unchanged in round 3.
- UNU is the one entity in this batch whose governing body is composed of
  **individuals appointed in a personal capacity**, not member-State
  representatives. It is intergovernmental in origin but not in
  composition, which is worth remembering when the "what counts as a
  governing body" rule is written.
- Contact: general contact page only (<https://unu.edu/about/contact-us>);
  no Council secretariat contact published on the Council page.
