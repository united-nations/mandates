# UNU — United Nations University

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNU is a research institute with a light governing body (the UNU Council). No centralized decision-tracking or mandate-registry system exists, and the Council's session records/decisions are not published as a public series. Only chronological annual reports are available.

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNU Annual Reports** — <https://unu.edu/about/annual-reports> — 14 reports (2012–2025), PDF, organized chronologically; narrative of UNU's work, not Council-decision-linked. Hosted on `unu.edu/sites/default/files/` (2025) and `collections.unu.edu/eserv/` (2012–2024).
- **UNU Collections** (institutional repository) — <https://collections.unu.edu/> — research publications and archived reports; not a governance/decision system.

## Notes

- Governing body: the **UNU Council** — 12 appointed members serving in individual capacity, the UNU Rector, and three ex officio members (UN Secretary-General, UNESCO Director-General, UNITAR Executive Director). Chair (2026): Prof. Sir Hilary Beckles. Holds two regular sessions per year; reports annually to the UN General Assembly, ECOSOC and the UNESCO Executive Board (<https://unu.edu/about/unu-council>, verified).
- Established by GA resolution 3081 (XXVIII), 6 December 1973; charter adopted 1973 (<https://unu.edu/about/unu-charter>); headquarters Tokyo.
- The Council's annual report to the General Assembly is issued in the UN document system, but UNU's own site does not surface Council decisions, agendas or session records as a browsable series — the closest public artifact is the yearly annual report.
- Contact: general contact page only (<https://unu.edu/about/contact-us>); no Council secretariat contact published on the Council page.
