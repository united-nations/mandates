# ILO — International Labour Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Governing Body Documents** (Decisions Database) — <https://www.ilo.org/governing-body/all-gb-documents>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — round 2 confirmed: decision pages/GB database carry no in-force/superseded status |
| 3 budget-linked | no — no system links decisions to amounts; decision pages show no financial fields (verified on a GB.352 decision page). Individual GB papers contain ad-hoc financing clauses ("financed from savings in Part I"), but only as document text |
| 4 implementation-linked | no evidence in round 1 |
| 5 results-linked | partial — standing "Follow-up to Governing Body decisions" report series gives the GB an overview of action taken per decision adopted at previous sessions (e.g. GB.343 first supplementary report; GB.352 decision noting follow-up to decisions Nov 2022–Jun 2024, next report Nov 2025); a document series, not a database |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **ILO Development Cooperation Dashboard** — <https://webapps.ilo.org/DevelopmentCooperationDashboard/> — budget/expenditure/projects for voluntary contributions (RBSA + XBDC), filterable by partner, country, P&B outcome, SDG; not linked to GB decisions.

## Notes

- GB documents filterable by session/type incl. "Record of decisions"; individual decision pages EN/FR/ES. NORMLEX covers labour standards, not GB decisions.
- The follow-up reports arguably fit point 4 (implementation) as much as point 5 (results) — they describe Office action taken, not outcomes achieved. Scored under 5 per round-2 remit; reconsider placement when consolidating.

## Open questions

- Cadence and coverage of the follow-up reports (all decisions vs. selected); whether they warrant point 4 credit instead; point 6 unassessed.
