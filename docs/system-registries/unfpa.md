## Notes

- Same joint-board machinery as UNDP. Nine tables are published, **2018–2026**, in six languages.
- **Most current of the batch.** The 2026, 2025, 2024, 2022 and 2021 tables all carry the "AS2026 – May 2026" stamp and the 2026 table's header reads "Last updated May 2026" — refreshed at the 2026 annual session, against UNDP's fourteen-month-old June 2025 refresh.
- Round 3 re-read the 2026 PDF in full. The header states the matrix "keeps track of the implementation of specific and time-bound requests **to UNFPA** contained in Executive Board decisions" — which is why UNCDF-directed requests appear only in UNDP's table, not this one.
- Status legend re-verified verbatim: "(1) GREEN ● signifies (a) 'completed' (action has been completed or decision is no longer relevant) or (b) 'ongoing' (action in progress, if a recurring item); (2) YELLOW ● signifies an action is 'partially completed' (action in progress, with a due date, if relevant)." This remains the survey's only explicit decision-obsolescence wording among the joint-board funds.
- The decisions page also carries the session compendia (e.g. "Decisions adopted by the Executive Board at its first regular session 2026 (DP/FPA/2026/12)", "Decisions adopted by the Executive Board during 2025 (DP/2026/2)").

## Governing bodies

- **Executive Board (UNDP, UNFPA and UNOPS)** — the sole governing body; approves the strategic plan (UNFPA Strategic Plan 2026–2029, DP/FPA/2025/9) and the integrated budget.
- **General Assembly — the round-2 removal is correct.** UNFPA has no UN regular-budget section (absent from the PPB 2027 entity list); it is wholly voluntarily funded and its budget is approved by the Executive Board. The GA created it and sets system-wide policy through the QCPR, which is what the `note` records.
