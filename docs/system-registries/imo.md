# IMO — International Maritime Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Index of IMO Resolutions** (Session Documents) — <https://www.imo.org/en/knowledgecentre/indexofimoresolutions/pages/default.aspx>
  — a hub of static per-organ pages: Assembly (A), Council (C), FAL, LEG,
  London Convention/Protocol (LDC/LC/LP), MEPC, MSC, TC. Each organ page
  lists resolutions grouped by decade and session pair, e.g. the Assembly
  page now runs to "2025-27 [s.34-35]" after the 34th Assembly (Dec 2025).

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — index coverage is current; full texts sit in meeting reports on IMODOCS (free public registration) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — round 3 re-confirmed on the live per-organ pages: resolutions are listed by session and decade with no in-force, revoked or replaced field, even though revocation is routine (A.658(16), A.739(18), A.789(19)) |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the results-based budget is itself adopted by Assembly resolution and structured by strategic directions; no system links individual resolutions to financial data |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — no per-resolution implementation status published |
| 5 results-linked | no — financial statements and budget documents only |
| 6 interoperability | deferred — static ASPX pages plus registration-gated IMODOCS; no export, no API |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

None found beyond the universal UN portals. IMODOCS (<https://docs.imo.org/>)
is document access, not a budget/results portal; GISIS covers
regulatory/shipping data, not mandates or budget.

## Notes

- **Round 3 (2026-08-13) link check: all live.**
  `imo.org/en/about/pages/structure.aspx` 200, Index of IMO Resolutions
  default page 200, per-organ pages 200 (assembly.aspx,
  council-c-default.aspx, msc.aspx, msc-2026-27.aspx). No redirects, no
  content drift; the Assembly index has been extended to cover the 34th
  Assembly.
- **Governing bodies re-cut under the round-3 rule.** IMO's structure page
  gives the tiers plainly:
  - **Assembly** — keep. "The highest Governing Body"; approves the work
    programme, votes the budget, determines financial arrangements.
  - **Council** — keep. Executive organ; between Assembly sessions it
    "performs all the functions of the Assembly" except recommendations to
    Governments on safety and pollution prevention.
  - **Main Committees** — keep, but **renamed**. "Five Main Committees" was
    a label, not a body name; it is now spelled out as MSC, MEPC, LEG, TC
    and FAL. Each consists of all Member States and each adopts resolutions
    in its own series (MSC.x, MEPC.x, …), including convention amendments.
    See UNCERTAINTIES: most of those resolutions are addressed to Member
    States and ships rather than to IMO itself.
  - **Sub-Committees** (HTW, III, NCSR, PPR, SDC, SSE, CCC) — excluded.
    They assist MSC and MEPC and do not adopt resolutions of their own.
- Third parties (USCG, classification societies) still maintain their own
  IMO-resolution lists with supersession notes — a standing signal that IMO
  publishes no status-bearing registry itself.
- The Council is being enlarged to 52 members by Convention amendment; this
  affects composition only, not the registry picture.
