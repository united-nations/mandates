# UNESCO — United Nations Educational, Scientific and Cultural Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Governing bodies

Bodies that adopt UNESCO's own mandates:

- **General Conference** — <https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/governing-bodies>
  — biennial, all Member States. "Determines the policies and main lines of
  work of the Organization, and takes decisions on its programmes"; adopts
  the Programme and Budget (C/5) and the Medium-Term Strategy (C/4) and
  adopts conventions and recommendations. JIU/REP/2026/1 (para. 28): it
  "sets the strategic direction, approves the programme and the budget, and
  adopts normative frameworks". Mandate-adopting: **yes** (resolutions).
- **Executive Board** — same page — 58 elected Member States, meets at
  least four times per two-year term. Prepares the General Conference
  agenda, reviews the work programme and budget proposals, is "responsible
  for the execution of the programme adopted by the General Conference",
  monitors implementation and makes recommendations. Mandate-adopting:
  **yes** (decisions).

No other UNESCO body adopts mandates for the Organization. The Commission
for Financial, Administrative and General Questions and the Budget and
Finance Committee (est. Nov 2024) only make recommendations to the
Executive Board (JIU/REP/2026/2, annex table (f)).

## Systems

- None scored. No centralized system. Two separate 2026 recommendations
  now call for one (see Notes).

## Notes

- **New since round 2 — JIU/REP/2026/1**, *Review of management and
  administration in UNESCO* (Joint Inspection Unit, 2026), **Recommendation
  1**: "Funds permitting, by the end of 2028, the Executive Board should
  request the Director-General to design and implement a centralized digital
  platform to track Executive Board decisions and General Conference
  resolutions, assign responsibilities, and monitor implementation in real
  time." The report suggests integrating the tool into **UNESCORE**, the
  user-facing portal layered on UNESCO's SAP ERP, and pairing it with
  mandatory progress reporting tied to ADG performance evaluations.
- The earlier trigger stands: 43 C/OEWG/2/INF.3.REV.2 (18 May 2026)
  recommends "a centralized decision-tracking system … aligned with the
  United Nations Mandate Register". The open-ended working group page
  (<https://www.unesco.org/en/governance/oewg>) carries documents up to
  mid-2026 (traffic-light report 24 April 2026; consolidated Member State
  inputs as of 18 May 2026) and **no outcome or decision after that date**.
- **Nothing has been established.** Re-checked 2026-08-13: the EB decisions
  page (page updated 22 July 2026) lists sessions up to the 225th (5–20
  October 2026) and forthcoming 226th–228th, still with no search, no
  database and no status marking.
- Per-session EB decisions finding aid exists via UNESDOC
  (`unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/decisions-exb`), so bare document access is
  arguably present despite the null score.

## Open questions

- Whether the 225th Executive Board session (October 2026) acts on JIU
  Recommendation 1 or the OEWG proposal — the first realistic decision point.
- Whether UNESCORE-hosted tracking, if built, would be public or
  Member-State-only (the JIU text frames it as empowering Member States,
  not the public).
