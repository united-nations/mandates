# WMO — World Meteorological Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Resolutions & Decisions Database** (Implementation Tracker) — <https://tools.wmo.int/wmo-resolutions/index.php>

## Notes

- tools.wmo.int combines an in-force resolutions/decisions database with a requests tracker: 545 Congress/EC requests with addressee, deadline and status (incl. "Delayed"). Closest peer to a full mandate registry.
- Round 2 re-verified the DB: filters for constituent body, session, instrument type, department/unit, LTG1–5, 20+ strategic objectives, 60+ keywords, year 1953–2025; strategic-objective filter connects to the plan taxonomy only, never to money.
