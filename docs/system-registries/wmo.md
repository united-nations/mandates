# WMO — World Meteorological Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Governing bodies

Bodies that adopt WMO's own mandates
(<https://wmo.int/about/governance>, re-verified 2026-08-13 — the recorded
`/about-wmo/governance` path still resolves, redirecting to `/about/governance`):

- **World Meteorological Congress** — "the general assembly and supreme body
  of the Organization", every four years. Determines general, technical,
  financial and staff regulations, **approves the long-term plan and the
  budget**, elects the President and appoints the Secretary-General.
  Mandate-adopting: **yes** (resolutions and decisions).
- **Executive Council** — annual; implements Congress decisions and adopts
  its own resolutions, decisions and recommendations between Congresses.
  Mandate-adopting: **yes**. Since 2020 it has issued 326 outputs (168
  resolutions, 123 decisions, 35 recommendations) — JIU/REP/2026/2.

**Borderline, not listed:** the **six regional associations** and the
**technical commissions** are constituent bodies that adopt their own
resolutions and decisions, tracked in the same database. JIU/REP/2026/2:
since 2019 the regional associations issued 180 resolutions, 207 decisions
and 3 recommendations — more resolutions than Congress itself — and the
technical commissions 58 resolutions, 146 decisions and 112 recommendations.
Flagged for the inclusion rule rather than added.

## Systems

- **Resolutions & Decisions Database** (Implementation Tracker) — <https://tools.wmo.int/wmo-resolutions/index.php>
  — live, re-verified 2026-08-13. **696 entries**, last update 8 December
  2025. Stated on the page: "The list of resolutions in force is publicly
  available both through this online database and as an annex to an official
  publication." Filters: constituent body (Congress, Executive Council,
  regional associations I–VI, others), session (Ext(2012) → Ext(2025)),
  instrument type (resolution / decision / recommendation), department/unit,
  Long-Term Goals LTG1–5 plus Governance, 20+ strategic objectives, 60+
  keywords, year 1953–2025. Per-entry "Progress"/"Recommendation" narratives
  ("Keep in force", "To be kept in force until the relevant publications are
  released on the e-library").
- **Requests tracker** — <https://tools.wmo.int/wmo-resolutions/requests.php>
  — live, re-verified 2026-08-13. **545 requests**, last update 8 December
  2025, covering requests made by Congress from Cg-Ext(2021) and by the
  Executive Council from EC-73, plus regional associations and technical
  commissions — 15 sessions in total, Cg-Ext(2021) through EC-79 (2025) and
  Cg-Ext(2025). Fields: addressee (60+ values: Secretary-General, Members,
  technical commissions, regional associations…), deadline (specific sessions
  EC-77/EC-78/EC-79/Cg-20 or "No Deadline") and status (including "Delayed").

Both counts and the December 2025 update date are unchanged from round 2 —
the two most consequential numbers in the survey are confirmed.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |

Total unchanged: **3**. WMO remains the only organization in the survey
combining an in-force resolutions database with per-request implementation
tracking.

## Notes

- **Independent corroboration, new this round: JIU/REP/2026/2**, *Review of
  governing body structures in the World Meteorological Organization* (Joint
  Inspection Unit, 2026) — a whole JIU report on WMO governance, which
  benchmarks "B.1.4 Decisions and resolutions are tracked and monitored for
  implementation" as **partially met**:
  - "Governing bodies usually track the implementation of their resolutions
    but not of their decisions";
  - the Executive Council reviewed the status of its decisions between its
    70th and 77th sessions (2018–2023) but **stopped doing so from EC-78
    (2024)**, "an approach that creates a monitoring and oversight gap";
  - as at November 2025, **112 Executive Council resolutions and decisions
    remain in force**; about 60% of regional-association resolutions and 50%
    of their decisions likewise;
  - "Regional associations should improve their current monitoring
    arrangements"; "Congress receives disparate reports on resolutions and
    decisions adopted by each constituent body".
- That caveat qualifies but does not overturn point 2: the in-force list is
  real, public and session-reviewed for **resolutions**; the gap is in
  decisions and in the consistency of regional-association monitoring.
- The strategic-objective filter connects to the plan taxonomy only, never
  to money — re-confirmed.
