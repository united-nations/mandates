# UNIDIR — United Nations Institute for Disarmament Research

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNIDIR is an autonomous research institute with a light governing body: the Secretary-General's **Advisory Board on Disarmament Matters (ABDM)**, which under article III of the UNIDIR statute "shall function as the Board" of Trustees. The Board's own decisions, agendas and session records are not published. What is public is a two-part **annual report series** — the Board's report to the General Assembly (`A/79/240`, `A/80/240` …) and the Director's report (`A/80/254` …), 2019–2025, PDF, on one page. That is a governance record series, not a decision-tracking or mandate-registry system (same treatment as UNU and UNITAR).

Detailed income and expenditure sit in the financial annexes of the Director's Reports.

## Notes

- Governing body: the **Advisory Board on Disarmament Matters** — established 1978, current mandate per GA decision 54/418 (1 December 1999). Members are chosen by the **Secretary-General** from all regions for knowledge and experience in disarmament and international security; ~15–20 members serving two-year terms, plus the UNIDIR Director as ex officio member (statute art. III.1). It **holds two sessions a year, alternating between New York and Geneva**; the chairmanship rotates by region yearly. Statute art. III.3 requires it to meet at least once a year (<https://disarmament.unoda.org/en/our-work/disarmament-bodies/advisory-board-disarmament-matters>, verified; <https://unidir.org/who-we-are/about-unidir/>, verified — 16 members listed, Chair H.E. D. B. Venkatesh Varma).
- The Board's role is triple: it advises the Secretary-General on arms limitation and disarmament, serves as UNIDIR's Board of Trustees, and advises on the UN Disarmament Information Programme. Only the second leg makes it UNIDIR's governing body.
- Statute functions (art. III.2): formulate operating principles; consider and adopt the annual work programme and budget estimates; recommend the regular-budget subvention; review finances; take the decisions necessary for the Institute's effectiveness. The Director is appointed by the Secretary-General after consultation with the Board (art. IV) — Dr. Robin Geiss since April 2021.
- UNIDIR's finances consolidate into `UN` in the CEB dataset; the Institute is funded overwhelmingly from voluntary contributions plus a modest regular-budget subvention.
- Contact: no Board-secretariat contact is published. General: <mailto:unidir@un.org>, +41 22 917 1141, Palais des Nations, 1211 Geneva 10 (<https://unidir.org/who-we-are/contact-us/>, verified). Media: <mailto:unidir.comms@un.org>.
