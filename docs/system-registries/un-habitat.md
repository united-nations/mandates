## Notes

- Web tracker mandated by Assembly res 2/10, updated semi-annually; shows per-resolution programmatic status, financial requirements and funding gaps — still the only budget-linked mandate case in the survey.
- Round 3 re-verified every headline figure directly on the live page and found them **unchanged** from round 2: total resource requirements $50,217K, total mobilized $23,453K, total funding gap $26,764K (53%); res 2/1 $1,085K / $384K / $701K (64%), "Under implementation". A per-resolution funding-level bar chart ranks all 15 resolutions from 0% to 100% funded.
- **Correction to round 2.** The status vocabulary is two labels, not three: "Under implementation" and "Implemented". "Cost-neutral" is *narrative text* inside the summary for res 1/1 ("the implementation of resolution 1/1 … is to be considered cost-neutral"), not a status value. The point-2 note has been corrected accordingly.
- Coverage is unchanged — Assembly sessions 1 (res 1/1–1/5) and 2 (res 2/1–2/10), 15 resolutions — and this is **not a coverage gap**: the resumed second session (29–30 May 2025) adopted the Strategic Plan 2026–2029 and elected the Assembly President and the 36 Executive Board members, but no new numbered resolutions, and the third session falls due in 2027 on the four-year cycle. Executive Board decisions remain outside the tracker.
- The "Implementation Summary" blocks are collapsed accordions and their text is not in the rendered page body, so round 2's narrative-outcome example (res 2/6, USD 24M channelled to local financing in 8 countries) could not be re-read this round. The summaries exist per resolution; `results = partial` is held on that structure.

## Governing bodies

- **UN-Habitat Assembly** — universal (193 States), meets every four years, examines and approves the strategic plan. Genuine mandating body.
- **Executive Board** — 36 States elected by the Assembly, meets three times a year; per UN-Habitat's own governance page it "approve[s] and oversee[s] the implementation of the annual programme of work and budget" and adopts "decisions on programmatic, operational and budgetary issues". Genuine mandating body.
- **Committee of Permanent Representatives** — meets twice every four years in open-ended manner; its recorded role is preparatory and advisory (it drafted the rules of procedure for the Assembly and the Executive Board under GA res 73/239). No evidence it adopts UN-Habitat mandates. **Recommended for removal** under the rule "only bodies that adopt the entity's own mandates"; flagged for human confirmation.
- **General Assembly / ECOSOC — the round-2 removal is questionable.** UN-Habitat is a Secretariat-administered programme with its own UN regular-budget section (Part V… Section 15, "Human settlements"), which only the General Assembly appropriates. In our own PPB 2027 extraction, 48 of the 78 legislative mandates cited for UN-Habitat are GA resolutions and 5 are ECOSOC resolutions (e.g. ECOSOC res 2025/25 on the UN Forum of Mayors) against 17 Assembly-sourced ones — that is, the GA supplies more of UN-Habitat's cited mandate corpus than its own Assembly does. GA res 73/239 is itself cited as a mandate. The GA is therefore not merely constitutive here.
