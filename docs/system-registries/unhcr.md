# UNHCR — Office of the United Nations High Commissioner for Refugees

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **ExCom documentation collection** (Documents Collection) — <https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/governance-and-oversight/executive-committee/excom-plenary-sessions> — online collection of Executive Committee documents browsable by session (reports, conclusions, decisions).
- Supplementary compilations: *A Thematic Compilation of Executive Committee Conclusions* (7th ed., 2014, PDF, <https://www.unhcr.org/media/thematic-compilation-executive-committee-conclusions-7th-edition>) and UNHCR's Refworld legal database, which index ExCom Conclusions on International Protection.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — ExCom session documents (agenda, conclusions, decisions, reports) compiled and publicly browsable by session; ExCom Conclusions additionally compiled thematically and in Refworld |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — ExCom Conclusions on International Protection are cumulative soft-law guidance; the thematic compilation reorders them by topic but nothing marks conclusions/decisions as in-force vs superseded/lapsed |
| 3 budget-linked | no — ExCom approves the annual programme budget target, but published budget/funding data (planning-funding-and-results portal) is organized by operation/country/strategic direction, not by ExCom conclusion or decision |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — implementation is reported at strategic/operational level (COMPASS results framework, Global Report), not per ExCom conclusion/decision |
| 5 results-linked | no — outcomes reported against the five Strategic Directions and COMPASS indicators, not against specific ExCom decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Planning, funding and results** (formerly Global Focus / reporting.unhcr.org, now merged into the main site) — <https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/planning-funding-and-results> — interactive budget/funding/expenditure dashboard (2012–2026) by operation, category and funding source, plus Global Appeal / Global Report; also published as IATI. Organized by operation and strategic plan, not by ExCom decisions. `reporting.unhcr.org` now 301-redirects here (verified 2026-07-19).
- **Operational Data Portal** — <https://data.unhcr.org/> — field/situation operational data.
- **Refugee Data Finder** — <https://www.unhcr.org/refugee-statistics> — population statistics.

## Notes

- ExCom (est. by ECOSOC res 672 (XXV), 1958; requested by GA res 1166 (XII)) is a subsidiary organ of the General Assembly; its documentation is issued in the GA `A/AC.96/-` series and its report goes to the GA Third Committee. It approves the annual programme budget target and adopts Conclusions on International Protection plus administrative/financial decisions. Rules of Procedure: A/AC.96/187/Rev.8.
- ExCom Secretariat contact confirmed: hqexcom@unhcr.org.
- The Thematic Compilation is a periodically re-issued PDF (7th ed. is the latest located, 2014), not a queryable system, and does not track in-force status — noted here, not scored.
- Scores 1 of 5 (documents only), same tier as WTO/IMO/etc.: a findable document collection with no active-mandate registry and no decision-linked budget/implementation/results.
