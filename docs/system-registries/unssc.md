# UNSSC — United Nations System Staff College

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored, and this is the thinnest case of the four research-and-training institutes. UNSSC's **Board of Governors** approves the work programme and budget, but the College publishes **no session records, no decisions and no board-document series at all** — only occasional news items announcing that a session concluded (e.g. "Board of Governors successfully concludes its 19th session", 22 February 2019). The only systematic public artifact is the annual report, addressed to donors and the public rather than to Board decisions.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | no — nothing published: no agendas, reports, minutes or decisions of the Board of Governors; the only public trace of a session is a news article. Weaker than UNU (annual reports only) and far weaker than UNITAR |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded tracking of any kind |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the Board "consider[s] the work programme and budget, on the basis of proposals submitted by the Director" (statute art. IV.7(b)); financial figures appear only inside annual reports, with no link to the approving decisions |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — no per-decision implementation tracking |
| 5 results-linked | no — annual reports narrate learning outputs and participant figures against the strategic plan, not against Board decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Annual reports** — <https://www.unssc.org/annual-reports> — 2018–2025, each as an interactive microsite plus a PDF (e.g. `annualreport2024.unssc.org`, `w02.unssc.org/ar22/`). Primary public reporting; organized by year and programme, not by Board decisions.
- **Donors** — <https://www.unssc.org/about/donors> — logos and names only (Compagnia di San Paolo, BMZ, German Federal Foreign Office, AICS, MAECI, Swiss FDFA, Irish Aid); no amounts, no dataset, no dashboard.

## Notes

- Governing body: the **Board of Governors** — statute art. IV. Composed of individuals drawn from the staff of CEB member organizations; the **CEB, through HLCM and HLCP, recommends individuals to the Secretary-General, who appoints a maximum of 9** for two-year terms (renewable once by one year), all at Director level or above. Ex officio: the CEB Secretary, the Director of the Staff College, and the Executive Director of UNITAR. It "shall meet at least once a year", adopts its own rules of procedure, and **submits an annual report to the CEB** — not to the General Assembly (art. IV.7(d)–(e)) (<https://www.unssc.org/sites/default/files/u3/unssc_statute.pdf>, verified; scanned PDF, text extracted).
- **Chairing discrepancy.** The statute (art. IV.5) says the Board "shall be chaired by the Deputy Secretary-General", who may designate any member to chair a session. In practice the Board is chaired by the **Chef de Cabinet of the Secretary-General** — currently Courtenay Rattray; the same was true in 2019 (Maria Luiza Ribeiro Viotti). Both the current and the 2019 board page describe the chair as the Chef de Cabinet (<https://www.unssc.org/about/board-governors>, verified).
- Current membership (verified): Chair Courtenay Rattray; members Atul Khare (DOS), Linda Maguire (UNDP), Raouf Mazou (UNHCR), Elizabeth Spehar (DPPA), Hannan Sulieman (UNICEF), Laura Thompson (ILO); ex officio Maaike Jansen (CEB Secretary), Michelle Gyles-McDonnough (UNITAR), Miguel Panadero (Acting Director, UNSSC).
- Established by GA resolution 55/207 (20 December 2000); statute approved by GA resolution 55/278 (12 July 2001), amended by ECOSOC resolution 2009/10; College operational from 1 January 2002. Located in Turin, with an office in Bonn.
- Contact: **no public email address is published anywhere on the site** — neither on `/contact-us` nor on `/contact-page`. Contact is via a web form; addresses are Viale Maestri del Lavoro 10, 10127 Turin, Italy, and UN Campus, Platz der Vereinten Nationen 1, 53113 Bonn, Germany (<https://www.unssc.org/contact-us>, verified).
