# Tech Bank — Technology Bank for the Least Developed Countries

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Council Documents** (Document Repository) — <https://www.un.org/technologybank/en/council-documents> — the complete series of Governing Council session documents, first session (2017) to tenth session (2025), each linked to its ODS record.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — every Council session is documented by four UN documents in the TBLDC/… series (provisional agenda, report on the work of the year, budget and programme of work, report of the Council on the session); the session report opens with a section "I. Decisions" listing what the Council adopted and requested (e.g. TBLDC/2026/4 paras 4–6) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — the series is chronological; nothing marks a Council decision as still in force, superseded or lapsed, and there is no compilation of standing Council decisions |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the Council approves one annual budget for the organization ($3.3 million for 2026, TBLDC/2026/3, ACABQ-reviewed); the budget document sets resources against strategic-plan pillars and objects of expenditure. Section V of the same document reproduces the Council's decisions, so budget and decisions travel in one document, but no decision or request is individually costed |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — the annual "Report on the work of the Technology Bank" (e.g. TBLDC/2026/2) is structured by strategic-plan pillars and activities; it does not report back on the previous session's Council requests, and no tracking table or status column exists anywhere in the series |
| 5 results-linked | no — results are reported org-wide against the strategic plan, in the Council work report and in the Secretary-General's annual report to the General Assembly (A/80/355), not against specific Council decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Key documents** — <https://www.un.org/technologybank/en/documents> — establishing resolution (A/RES/71/251), Charter (A/71/363), strategic plans and the Secretary-General's reports on the work of the Technology Bank (A/76/272, A/79/239, A/80/355); org-wide reporting to the General Assembly, not linked to Council decisions.
- No financial-transparency portal of its own was found; the Technology Bank's accounts are kept by the UN Secretariat under the UN Financial Regulations and Rules and audited by the UN Board of Auditors (Charter, art. 8).

## Notes

- **Governing body: the Council** ("Governing Council" in everyday use). Charter (A/71/363) art. 5: 13 members who are experts in science, technology and innovation and in development cooperation, **appointed by the Secretary-General**, serving **in their individual capacity**, normally for **three-year terms**, renewable; among them one appointed with the concurrence of the host country and one representing the Secretary-General. The Council elects its Chair, adopts its own rules of procedure (TBLDC/2017/2) and **meets at least once a year**; UNCTAD, UNDP, UNESCO, the World Bank and WIPO have a standing observer invitation.
- Council functions (art. 5(g)): approve the multi-year strategic plan, the annual work programme, operational principles, policies and guidelines and the **budget**; adopt such decisions and take measures as necessary; consider the Managing Director's reports; report annually to the General Assembly through the Secretary-General; create subsidiary bodies. Art. 8(g): the budget estimates go to the Council for approval together with ACABQ comments, and the approved budget is **transmitted to the General Assembly** with the Council's report.
- Established by **General Assembly resolution 71/251** (23 December 2016), which adopted the Charter and affirms the Technology Bank as **a subsidiary organ of the General Assembly**. The Assembly is therefore constitutive, and receives the budget and the annual report for information; it does not approve them. Its LDC follow-up resolutions (79/218, 80/150) commission the Secretary-General's report on the Technology Bank's work.
- Practice observed at the tenth session (virtual, 11–12 December 2025): the Council adopted the agenda, the minutes of the midyear meeting, the report to the General Assembly, the budget and programme of work for 2026 and the subcommittee report on resource mobilization; issued several requests to the Technology Bank (comprehensive assessment of technology needs assessments with a road map; country-specific resource mobilization strategy with time-bound targets; enhanced visibility at UN conferences). The Council also met on 30–31 January and 21 July 2025, so it convenes more often than the one annual session.
- Chair for 2024–2026: Hasan Mandal (President of TÜBİTAK). Acting Managing Director: Roland Mollerus, who is concurrently Director of OHRLLS. Headquarters: TÜBİTAK Gebze Campus, Gebze, Kocaeli, Türkiye.
- A resource-mobilization **subcommittee** of the Council exists and reports to it; it is a subsidiary of the governing body, not a governing body itself.

## Open questions

- Point 6 interoperability (deferred to a later round).
- The `documents` point is a borderline call of the same kind flagged for UNAIDS, UNITAR, UNCCD and ITC: a complete, well-organized per-session document series with an explicit "Decisions" section, but not a queryable system. Scored `true` here for the completeness of the series (ten sessions, all in ODS) and because the decisions are set out as decisions, not buried in a narrative.
- Whether the Council's rules of procedure (TBLDC/2017/2) provide for any register of standing decisions — the rules were not read in full in this round.
