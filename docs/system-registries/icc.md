# ICC — International Criminal Court

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **ASP Resolutions** (Session Documents) — <https://asp.icc-cpi.int/resolutions>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — re-verified round 3; per-session resolution listings from the 1st session (2002–2003) to the **24th session (2025–2026)**, plus a 2025 special session |
| 2 mandates (active) | no system — the nuance stands and was re-verified against the current text: the annual omnibus resolution carries Annex I "Mandates of the Assembly of States Parties for the intersessional period", a refreshed list of currently-active ASP mandates (document practice, not a registry) |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the ASP adopts an annual budget resolution with programme-level appropriations (Proposed Programme Budget 2026 = ICC-ASP/24/10, EUR 197.5M), but no system links mandates to their resourcing |
| 4 implementation-linked | no |
| 5 results-linked | no system — but a paper-based mandate cycle exists: Bureau/facilitator reports (e.g. ICC-ASP/24/28) report back on specific mandates from the omnibus-resolution annex; published as session documents only |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Notes

- Round 3 link check: `asp.icc-cpi.int/`, `/resolutions`, `/secretariat-contact` and `/CBF` all return HTTP 200 to a browser-style client (WebFetch is still refused with 403; the earlier "verified via search-indexed copies" caveat no longer applies to these four).
- **Omnibus annex re-confirmed, judgement unchanged.** The current omnibus is **ICC-ASP/24/Res.6, "Strengthening the International Criminal Court and the Assembly of States Parties"** (adopted by consensus 5 December 2025, 27 pages). Its operative text instructs the organs "to comply… with the mandates contained in annex I to the present resolution", and Annex I is headed "Mandates of the Assembly of States Parties for the intersessional period". The annex was read directly: it is a numbered list by theme (universality, Agreement on Privileges and Immunities, cooperation, …), each item naming the addressee (Bureau, Secretariat, Court, working groups) and, for most, an explicit report-back — "to report thereon to the Assembly at its twenty-fifth session".
- So the annex is a genuine, yearly-refreshed active-mandate list with per-mandate follow-up — **but it is a PDF annex to one resolution, not a queryable system**, and nothing in the ASP web estate has turned it into one. Deliberately not scored; this is the strongest "mandate registry on paper" case found anywhere in the survey and is worth citing as such.
