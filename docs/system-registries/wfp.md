## Governing bodies

- **Executive Board** — the sole governing body, and genuinely so. Verified from WFP's own March 2026 governance induction deck (WFP-0000161664) and the "About the Board" page: 36 members, of which **ECOSOC elects 18 and the FAO Council elects 18**, for three-year terms; the Board "review[s], modif[ies] as necessary and approve[s] programmes, management plan budgets, projects and activities submitted to it by the Executive Director"; its stated accountability line is "Accountability to the FAO Council and ECOSOC", to which it reports annually.
- **General Assembly — the round-2 removal is correct.** WFP has no UN regular-budget section (absent from the PPB 2027 entity list) and is wholly voluntarily funded. The GA does not appear as a governance tier in WFP's own governance chart; it figures only as the pool of "States Members of the UN General Assembly" from which ECOSOC elects.
- **Open point on ECOSOC and the FAO Council.** Both are real tiers — they constitute the Board and receive its annual report — but neither adopts WFP programme mandates, so under the "only bodies that adopt the entity's own mandates" rule neither is listed. WFP is the batch's clearest case of a jointly governed entity (UN/FAO); whether the FAO side should appear at all is flagged for the inclusion rule. The `note` has been rewritten to record the ECOSOC/FAO Council relationship rather than the QCPR alone, which is only half of WFP's situation.
