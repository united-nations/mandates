# WFP — World Food Programme

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Executive Board Documents** (Documents Database) — <https://executiveboard.wfp.org/documents>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | no evidence in round 1 |
| 3 budget-linked | no — no public linkage of Board decisions to budget amounts; oversight-recommendation dashboards and the Board's financial/performance portal are members-only |
| 4 implementation-linked | no evidence in round 1 |
| 5 results-linked | no — the Annual Performance Report reports against the strategic plan / corporate results framework (strategic results, outcomes, indicators), not against Board decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Annual Performance Report dashboards** — interactive APR dashboards on analytics.wfp.org, linked per year from <https://www.wfp.org/publications/annual-performance-report> — global/regional/country results vs the corporate results framework.

## Notes

- Oversight-recommendation dashboards exist but are members-only; the Board also has a members-only portal with financial and performance information. WFP is jointly governed (UN/FAO).
- Documents database lists "Decisions"/"Resolutions" as document types, but most records show as "Restricted document" without login.

## Open questions

- Whether members-only dashboards link recommendations/decisions to budget — unverifiable publicly.
- Point 6 interoperability (deferred to a later round).
