# CTBTO — Preparatory Commission for the Comprehensive Nuclear-Test-Ban Treaty Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. Preparatory Commission documents are distributed through a members-only system; no public compilation. Re-verified round 3.

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- None found beyond the universal UN portals. Public site offers Annual Reports, brochures/factsheets, a Status of Assessed Contributions page and a "Legal Resources" page for member States; no budget-document or decisions portal.

## Notes

- PrepCom documents distributed via the restricted Experts Communication System; public site has only press releases, regulations PDFs, annual reports.
- Round 2: budget figures (e.g. US$139.31M for 2026) appear on org pages and in historical press releases about session outcomes, never as linked decision documents.
- Round 3 link check: `/our-mission/the-organization/the-preparatory-commission`, `/our-mission/the-organization/the-provisional-technical-secretariat`, `/resources`, `/contact-us` all HTTP 200. `/press-centre/documents` (indexed by search engines) and `/our-mission/the-organization/schedule-of-sessions` are 404; the sessions schedule moved to `/news-and-events/schedule-of-sessions`.
- Round 3 contact: `https://www.ctbto.org/contact-us` is a real contact page — postal address "CTBTO Preparatory Commission, Vienna International Centre, P.O. Box 1200, 1400 Vienna, Austria", phone "+43 1 26030-0", and a routed enquiry form (categories: Donor Resources, Employment/HR, Experts Communications System, Legal, Media & Press, Other, Procurement, Protocol, vDec, Website). No separate Preparatory Commission / policy-making-organs contact is published. This is a better contact than the Provisional Technical Secretariat page currently recorded, which carries no contact details.
