# ICAO — International Civil Aviation Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Assembly Resolutions in Force** (Resolutions in Force) — <https://www.icao.int/events/a41/resolutions>
  — live, and still the target of the site-wide "Resolutions in Force" item
  in ICAO's own top navigation. Content is Doc 10184, *as of 7 October 2022*
  (close of the 41st Assembly).

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — resolutions in force in six languages, plus reservations; A42 texts published separately as a provisional PDF |
| 2 mandates (active) | **partial** (round 3 downgrade, was `yes`) — Doc 10184 is by construction an in-force consolidation, but it is a triennial **PDF publication**, not a queryable system, which is exactly the weak form the scheme calls `'partial'` (same treatment as UNFCCC's "SB Mandates" compilation). It is also now one full session out of date: the 42nd Assembly (22 Sept – 3 Oct 2025) adopted resolutions A42-1…A42-27, and no post-A42 edition of Doc 10184 was found |
| 3 budget-linked | no — Budget Doc 10229 (2026–2028) opens with the budget Assembly resolution (A42-27) and prioritizes "projects and activities mandated by the Assembly and/or Council", but amounts are organized by Strategic Goals/programmes with no per-resolution figures |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — no per-resolution implementation reporting found in three rounds |
| 5 results-linked | no — performance reporting runs against the Business Plan / Strategic Objectives framework; the triennial consolidation of resolutions in force is an editorial review, not results reporting |
| 6 interoperability | deferred — PDF only; no download bundle, no API |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

None found beyond the universal UN portals. Budget and results remain
Doc-series PDFs (Doc 10229, Doc 10174); there is no ICAO transparency or
results portal.

## Notes

- **Round 3 (2026-08-13) link check — one dead entry, now corrected.**
  - `icao.int/events/a41/resolutions` — 200, unchanged content.
  - `icao.int/icao-assembly` — 200. States the Assembly meets at least once
    every three years and "review[s] and adopt[s] the work programme
    priorities and budget for ICAO".
  - `icao.int/icao-council` — 200. 36 States; "Secretariat oversight, and …
    the ongoing diplomatic and technical decision making" between Assemblies.
  - `icao.int/committees-conference` — 200 but **wrong body entirely**: it is
    an archival page about the committees of the *1944 Chicago Conference*
    (Committee I on the Multilateral Aviation Convention, Committee IV on
    the Interim Council, Committee on Credentials, …), constituted at the
    second plenary session on 2 November 1944. It is not an ICAO organ and
    never was. **Removed from the governing-body list.**
- **A42 is documented, but not consolidated.** The session page
  <https://www.icao.int/events/assembly-42nd-session> (also reachable as
  `icao.int/A42`) is live and has a Resolutions tab
  (`/events/assembly-42nd-session/Resolutions`) which currently renders no
  resolution files. The adopted texts exist only as the provisional edition
  PDF (1.7 MB, October 2025):
  `icao.int/sites/default/files/Meetings/a42/Documents/Resolutions/a42_res_prov_en.pdf`.
  So the round-2 open question resolves as: A42 resolutions published, but
  the in-force consolidation has not caught up.
- **Governing bodies under the round-3 rule:** Assembly and Council only.
  The Air Navigation Commission and the Council's committees (Air
  Transport, Finance, Legal, Unlawful Interference, Technical Cooperation,
  HR) prepare and recommend to the Council — advisory, so excluded.
