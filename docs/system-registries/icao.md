# ICAO — International Civil Aviation Organization

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Assembly Resolutions in Force** (Resolutions in Force) — <https://www.icao.int/events/a41/resolutions>

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes |
| 2 mandates (active) | yes |
| 3 budget-linked | no — Budget Doc 10229 (2026–2028) opens with the budget Assembly resolution (A42-27) and its prioritization "focused on projects and activities mandated by the Assembly and/or Council", but amounts are organized by Strategic Goals/programmes with no per-resolution figures (verified in the PDF text) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

None found beyond the universal UN portals. Budget and results are published as PDF Doc-series documents (e.g. Doc 10229, Doc 10174), not as an open portal.

## Notes

- Doc 10184 is a periodic consolidated publication of Assembly resolutions in force (41st edition; 42nd in preparation), not a database.
- Round 2: the 42nd Assembly session has since adopted resolutions (A42-27 is the 2026–2028 budget resolution, quoted in Doc 10229); the round-1 flagship URL is the A41 resolutions page — check for an A42 successor page when next verified.
- Budget documents state the mandate-to-programme principle (each programme "mandated either by an Assembly Resolution and/or a Council Decision", per Assembly working papers) without publishing the mapping — near-miss on point 3.
