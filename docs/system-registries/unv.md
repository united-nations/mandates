# UNV — United Nations Volunteers programme

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **Decision Tracking Tables** (Decision Tracking) — <https://www.undp.org/executive-board/decisions-of-the-board> — the joint UNDP/UNFPA/UNOPS Executive Board machinery (see UNDP); verified to carry UNV rows, with "UNV" as the responsible unit.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — the Board compiles its decisions per session and per year (e.g. the 2022 compendium DP/2023/4, republished by UNV itself), and UNV-specific decisions are individually findable (2019/9, 2022/9, 2024/17, 2025/16) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — tracking tables mark each request Completed / Ongoing / In Progress / Partially Completed; per-request status only, no current-vs-replaced registry of decisions |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the tables (Decision / Category / Responsible divisions / Request / Deadline / Progress and follow-up actions / Status) carry no financial amounts; UNV's financial overview is an annex to the annual report, organized by fund and cost category, not by decision |
| 4 implementation-linked | yes — unlike UNOPS, UNV's requests are inside the UNDP tracking table: decision 2022/9 paras 6–7 and decision 2024/17 paras 3 and 6 are assigned to "UNV" as responsible division, with deadline "Ongoing", a Progress and follow-up actions entry and status "In Progress" (verified in the 2022 and 2024 tables) |
| 5 results-linked | no — the Progress column records follow-up actions taken, not outcomes; results are reported against the Strategic Framework in the Administrator's annual report |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **UNV annual reports** — <https://www.unv.org/annual-reports> — org-wide results and volunteer/financial statistics per year; organized by Strategic Framework outcomes, not by Board decisions.
- **Knowledge Portal on Volunteerism** — <https://knowledge.unv.org/> — volunteering laws, policies, measurement and evidence library; subject-matter portal, no mandate or finance linkage.
- **UNDP Transparency Portal** — <https://open.undp.org> — the administering organization's IATI-based project/budget portal (see UNDP); no UNV decision linkage.

## Notes

- Governing body: the **Executive Board of UNDP, UNFPA and UNOPS** (shared ref `undp-board`). UNV is administered by UNDP and "reports to the UNDP/UNFPA/UNOPS Executive Board" (unv.org/about-unv/who-we-are). No separate board, council or trustees of its own were found; the Special Voluntary Fund, created by General Assembly resolution 2659 (XXV), is likewise reported on to that Board.
- The Board treats UNV as a standing agenda item at its **annual session** (June) and adopts a **UNV-specific decision each year**: 2019/9, 2022/9, 2024/17, 2025/16 ("United Nations Volunteers programme: annual report of the Administrator"). Each report closes with "Elements of a decision".
- **Reporting cycle: annual since decision 2018/8.** DP/2025/19 para 1: "In accordance with Executive Board decision 2018/8, this report details the United Nations Volunteers (UNV) programme's 2024 performance." DP/2018/14 para 47(d) states UNV "has reported biennially to Board since 1988" and invited the Board to request annual reporting "(as it did prior to 1988)". Report series: DP/2018/14, DP/2021/25, DP/2022/24, DP/2023/23, DP/2024/23, DP/2025/19, DP/2026/23.
- The General Assembly created UNV (resolution 2659 (XXV), 1970) and addresses it in its volunteerism resolutions — 76/131, and 78/127 which "invites the United Nations Volunteers programme … to facilitate implementation of the International Year" and to inform the Assembly at its eighty-second session — but it neither approves UNV's programme nor appropriates its budget. Not listed as a governing body, on the same reading applied to UNDP/UNCDF.
- undp.org returns 403 to non-browser clients; the tracking tables, decision compendia and Board reports were verified by downloading the PDFs directly (2022 table published Aug 2023, 2024 table published June 2025, annual-session-2025 decision compilation DP/2025/21).
