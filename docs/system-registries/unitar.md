# UNITAR — United Nations Institute for Training and Research

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNITAR is a training/research institute with a light governing body (the Board of Trustees). It publishes a complete, chronological series of **Board of Trustees session reports** (sessions 40–66, 2002–2025, symboled e.g. `UNITAR/BT/66/5`) plus Finance Committee reports — but this is a static PDF document series, not a queryable decision-tracking or mandate-registry system. Treated as a governance record series, not scored (mirrors UNESCO's per-session-decisions treatment).

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | no (borderline) — Board session reports are compiled and publicly findable as a complete symboled PDF series, which is stronger than most null-scored organizations; but it is a webpage list of PDFs, not a system, so it stays below the bar (see UNCERTAINTIES) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — Board reports narrate decisions per session; no in-force/superseded status or active-mandate registry |
| 3 budget-linked | no — the Board "adopts the budget" and a Finance Committee reports on financials, but budget is not linked to specific Board decisions in any system; Finance Committee reports (18, 2009–2025) are a separate PDF series |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — no per-decision implementation tracking |
| 5 results-linked | no — annual Results Reports and biennial Programme Performance Reports are organized around the "six high-level programme areas of the strategic framework" and trained-beneficiary outputs, not against specific Board decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **Board of Trustees session reports** — <https://unitar.org/about/unitar/governance/board-trustees/board-reports> — ~40 session reports (40th–66th, 2002–2025) + 18 Finance Committee reports, all PDF. The governance record, but a document series not a system.
- **Results Reports and other reports** — <https://unitar.org/results-evidence-learning/results-reports-and-other-reports> — annual Results Reports (2014–2025, web + PDF) and biennial Programme Performance Reports (back to 2008–2009); organized by strategic-framework programme areas, not by Board decisions.

## Notes

- Governing body: the **Board of Trustees** — distinguished trustees appointed by the UN Secretary-General in consultation with the Presidents of the GA and ECOSOC (max two consecutive three-year terms), plus ex officio members (SG, GA President, ECOSOC President, UNITAR Executive Director). Formulates policies, approves the work programme, adopts the budget, reviews staffing/financing. Meets in regular session at least once a year; reports to the GA and ECOSOC (<https://unitar.org/about/unitar/governance/board-trustees>, verified).
- Board Secretary: **Brook Boyer** — <mailto:BOT@unitar.org>; Palais des Nations, CH-1211 Geneva 10, Switzerland (verified on Board page).
- Statute and Rules of Procedure of the Board are published (statute PDF at `/sites/default/files/media/publication/doc/unitar-statute-english-s.pdf`; Rules of Procedure revised after 63rd session).
- Governance hub: <https://unitar.org/about/unitar/governance>.
