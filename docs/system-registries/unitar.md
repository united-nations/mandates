# UNITAR — United Nations Institute for Training and Research

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- None scored. UNITAR is a training/research institute with a light governing body (the Board of Trustees). It publishes a complete, chronological series of **Board of Trustees session reports** (sessions 40–66, 2002–2025, symboled e.g. `UNITAR/BT/66/5`) plus Finance Committee reports — but this is a static PDF document series, not a queryable decision-tracking or mandate-registry system. Treated as a governance record series, not scored (mirrors UNESCO's per-session-decisions treatment).

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |

| 2 mandates (active) | no — Board reports narrate decisions per session; no in-force/superseded status or active-mandate registry |

## Governing bodies — re-verified, removal CORRECT

The **Board of Trustees** is the governing body and the GA/ECOSOC removal
is **correct**. Board page re-verified live 13 Aug 2026
(<https://unitar.org/about/unitar/governance/board-trustees>): the Board
"formulates principles and policies to govern the Institute's activities
and operations; **approves the work programme; adopts the budget**", and
reviews staffing structure and financing methods "to ensure … the
Institute's autonomous character within the framework of the United
Nations". Currently 13 trustees, appointed by the UN Secretary-General in
consultation with the Presidents of the GA and ECOSOC (max two
consecutive three-year terms), plus ex officio members (SG, GA President,
ECOSOC President, UNITAR Executive Director). Meets at least annually,
sometimes outside Geneva. A **Finance Committee** supports it by reviewing
budgets and financial statements.

Supporting facts for the removal:

- UNITAR has **no UN programme-budget section**
  (`data/references/ppb_sections_and_entities.tsv`), so the GA
  appropriates nothing for it; it is funded by voluntary contributions and
  training fees. Correspondingly it carries no `this-site` Mandate Source
  Registry entry.
- The GA and ECOSOC Presidents sit on the Board **ex officio**, and the
  Board *reports* to the GA and ECOSOC — a reporting and appointment
  relationship, not a governing one.

Note the same composition subtlety as UNU: the appointed trustees serve in
a personal capacity rather than as member-State representatives.

## Open questions

  earn **documents = partial** (a document series in weak form). Held at
  `no` here to stay consistent with UNESCO (per-session decisions,
