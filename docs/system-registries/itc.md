# ITC — International Trade Centre

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

ITC is a joint subsidiary organ of the World Trade Organization (WTO) and
the United Nations (through UNCTAD) — the only fully joint UN/WTO agency,
which is why its governance runs through two parent tracks at once.

## Systems

- None scored. No decision-tracking system. ITC's governing body, the
  **Joint Advisory Group (JAG)**, is advisory: it meets annually, examines
  the Executive Director's Annual Report, and makes **recommendations** to
  the UNCTAD Trade and Development Board and the WTO Committee on Trade and
  Development. There is no in-force/superseded register of those
  recommendations and no per-decision implementation or budget tracking.

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | no — arguably present: the governance pages publish per-session JAG materials (agenda, Annual Report, post-session JAG report/conclusions, list of participants) with a 2019–2026 session archive, but this is a per-session document listing of advisory conclusions, not a compiled/searchable decision registry |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded status for JAG recommendations |
| 3 budget-linked | no — Regular Budget is funded in equal parts by the UN and WTO and approved by the UN General Assembly and the WTO General Council; the Open Data Portal carries extra-budgetary (IATI) money by project, neither linked to specific JAG recommendations |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — reporting is via the annual Executive Director report against the Strategic Plan and biannual CCITF reports against operational plans, not per JAG recommendation |
| 5 results-linked | no — results-based management dashboard and Independent Evaluation Unit report at strategic-plan / project level, not against specific decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **ITC Open Data Portal (Beta)** — <https://open.intracen.org/> — IATI-based; extra-budgetary project data by recipient country/region, funder, sector, impact area and SDG (status 30.06.2025, ~90% of XB funds). Organized by project, not by JAG recommendations. Bulk download via the IATI registry.
- **ITC on the IATI Registry** — <https://www.iatiregistry.org/publisher/itc> — machine-readable activity data (source of the Open Data Portal).

## Notes

- Governance overview verified on the governance page (<https://www.intracen.org/about-us/governance>): parent bodies (UN via UNCTAD + WTO), JAG, CCITF (Consultative Committee of the ITC Trust Fund), Board of Auditors, JIU and OIOS oversight. Corporate documents offered as individual PDFs (Strategic Plan 2026–2030, Operational Plan 2026, Proposed Programme Budget, Annual Report) — a document set, not a system.
- JAG page verified (<https://www.intracen.org/about-us/governance/the-joint-advisory-group-jag>): 60th session on 2 July 2026 at the WTO in Geneva; related documents = agenda, Annual Reports, Strategic Plan, JAG Reports (posted after the meeting), list of participants; "Previous JAG sessions" archive covers 2019–2025.
- ITC is subject to the UN Financial Regulations and Rules and audited by the UN Board of Auditors (unqualified opinions; IPSAS-compliant).
- Contacts: JAG secretariat <jag@intracen.org> (Orlane Robert); Open Data <opendata@intracen.org>.

## Open questions

- Whether ITC ever compiles JAG recommendations into a cross-session, statused list (the raw material exists in the Annual Reports and JAG reports but is not assembled as a register).
- Whether the Open Data Portal (currently Beta, XB-only) will extend to Regular Budget or link money to governing decisions.
- Point 6 interoperability (deferred to a later round); the Open Data Portal exposes an internal JSON API and IATI download worth checking then.
