# UNCCD — United Nations Convention to Combat Desertification Secretariat

Dossier on registry-like systems for governing-body mandates. Part of the
survey behind the `/system` mandate-registry column; taxonomy and scoring
scheme in [`../un-system-registries.md`](../un-system-registries.md).

## Systems

- **COP decisions** (Decisions compilation) — <https://www.unccd.int/convention/cop-decisions> — decisions of the sixteen COP sessions, listed per session as PDFs (e.g. 1/COP.16 … 37/COP.16), six languages.
- **Official documents** — <https://www.unccd.int/convention/official-documents> — per-session PDF listings for the COP and its two subsidiary bodies, the CRIC (Committee for the Review of the Implementation of the Convention) and the CST (Committee on Science and Technology).

## Point assessment

| Point | Assessment |
| --- | --- |
| 1 documents | yes — COP/CRIC/CST decision and document texts are compiled per session and publicly findable on the official site; note this is a per-session document listing, not a queryable/full-text system (no cross-decision search facets, so interoperability is low) |
| 2 mandates (active) | no — no in-force/superseded tracking anywhere; only per-session decision listings (same pattern as UNESCO/WTO) |
| 3 budget-linked | no — decisions are not linked to budget/financial information; the Convention's budget is adopted per COP but published as documents, not per decision |
| 4 implementation-linked | no — implementation is reviewed via the CRIC through the PRAIS national-reporting system, organized by the five Strategic Objectives / LDN indicators per country, not against specific COP decisions |
| 5 results-linked | no — PRAIS reports SO/LDN indicator results by country and region, not outcomes against specific decisions |
| 6 interoperability | deferred (later round) |

## Related portals (not linked to mandates)

- **PRAIS 4 reporting platform** — <https://reporting.unccd.int> (info: <https://www.unccd.int/data-knowledge/unccd-national-reporting-process>) — Performance Review and Assessment of Implementation System; centralized database of national reports on the 2018–2030 Strategic Framework (five Strategic Objectives, LDN target zones, financial-flow tracking). Organized by country and strategic-objective indicator, not by COP decisions; relaunched (PRAIS 4) August 2025 for the 2026 reporting cycle.

## Notes

- UNCCD is **absent from the UN system chart dataset** — flagged as an open question whether it belongs on the `/system` page at all (see Uncertainties in the return).
- Governing body: Conference of the Parties (COP), biennial since 2001 (COP16 Dec 2024; COP17 Ulaanbaatar Aug 2026), with the CRIC (review) and CST (science) subsidiary bodies. Grouping: `related-organization`.
- Borderline point-1 call: the decision access is a set of per-session PDF pages rather than a real database; scored `true` on the "compiled and publicly findable" reading (as WTO/UNDP were), but it is weaker than a Documents-Online-style system — this only affects the interoperability point, deferred.
