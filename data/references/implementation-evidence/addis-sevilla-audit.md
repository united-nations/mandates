The latter have `locator.position`141–145 and all received explicit no-match records because the development-cooperation report does not report implementation of the TFM task team, platform, annual forum or HLPF input. Every record preserves exact registry source text and structural scope. Existing curated evidence and fine-grained excerpts were retained.

Important additions for Addis include: INFF adoption; vulnerable-country financing constraints; national/public development banks; philanthropy; blended-finance volumes, impact and fair risk-sharing; South-South and triangular cooperation; shrinking grant and programmable aid shares; climate finance and measurement; MDB balance-sheet reform; country-led coordination and UN development-system alignment; financing-flow data; multidimensional vulnerability/Beyond GDP; and DCF follow-up. Other sectoral, tax, debt-restructuring, intellectual-property or specialized institutional provisions are not mapped merely because the report discusses financing generally.

Important additions for Sevilla include: the overall implementation assessment, all action-area follow-up topics within OP3, Platform for Action coalitions and specific initiatives, UN/MDB coordination (a partial OP5 correspondence), prospective DCF follow-up within OP9 and vulnerable-country gaps. OPs1,6,12,13 and 15 have no matching numbered report passage. For OP6 the report repeatedly cites Financing for Sustainable Development Report2026 in footnotes; these bibliographic references establish that a report is cited but do not assess the requested annual IATF reporting process, accessibility changes or depth of reporting, and were not converted into invented numbered paragraphs. OP12 trust-fund contributions and OP13 Office/expert collaboration are not described. Formal agenda/endorsement language is not relabelled as implementation.

Evidence links distinguish activities, reported outputs/indicators, analytical findings, contextual gaps, recommendations and reporting duties. Reported regression (ODA cuts, declining grants, weak coordination, untaken governance reforms) is retained rather than omitted. Potential lending capacity is identified as potential; pending governance/architecture changes and future conferences are not classified as completed reforms. Explicit limitations travel in mapping interpretations. A matched status means at least one substantive correspondence, not fulfillment of all clauses. A no-match means no sufficiently specific passage in this selected report, not non-implementation or absence of evidence in other documents.

Validation checks assert that all component highlights occur exactly once in their source provision; all report excerpts occur exactly once in their full report paragraph; results have an output/indicator level and other kinds do not; and every source provision has a matched/no-match outcome. Source order is retained, including the TFM children. Full report paragraph text and original PDF page references remain available for context.
